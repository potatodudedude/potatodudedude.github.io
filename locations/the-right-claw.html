The Right Claw

Originally a small fishing town, the right claw came to prominence after the founding of the Aquatine Empire in 703. The town gets its name from the geography it inhabits, looking like a claw on the right side of the map. The town holds economic significance in the empire, being the largest settlement with a port within the borders.

The latest governor of the town was Pranav Mahit. The local captain of the guard was Hong Li Ma.

During the Tyranny of the Stars, the Right Claw remained one of only two Aquatine settlements that held out against the Nebulans until the end of the invasion, the other being the capital of Stonebasin. Despite the military victory, due to the surrounding lands being devastated by Stardust and Marander Gas, as well as the logistics of getting supplies to the town having long since broken into nothing, the Right Claw was abandoned.

A memorial to The Canaries was built within the town on request of Captain Li Ma, a reminder of their sacrifice. A blue oak sapling is planted in the middle of the memorial, a small beacon of hope in a wasteland.

Places that used to exist before the Nebulan invasion

Blacksmith

The blacksmith sells all weapons, apart from the specialist faction weapons.
The melee weapons not sold are:

  • One-handed Flails / Two-handed Flails (Kingdom of Numeria)
  • One-handed Falces / Two-handed Falces (Wingard Collective)
  • Numerian Blades (Conclave of the Steel Faeries)
  • Berserker Chain (Steelscale Clan)
    The ranged weapons not sold are:
  • Arbalest (Kingdom of Numeria)
  • Hand Crossbows (Wingard Collective)
  • Muskets / Pistols (Conclave of the Steel Faeries)
  • Amber Lances (Steelscale Clan)
    The blacksmith will only sell you moonspades and repeating crossbows with permission from the local rulers.

The blacksmith also sells:

  • All body armours and helmets apart from faerian items.
  • Fletching kits
  • Smithing tools
  • Metal bars

Communal Church

The local communal church has shrines to:

  • Tamira
  • Kiawan
  • New Illuminetres

General Traders

At the bustling markets, you can buy:

  • Bottles
  • Cloth / Linen / Wool
  • Feathers
  • Hide
  • Leather
  • Timber
  • All food items except:
    • Faerian items, moonshine and concentrated alcohol.
    • Due to shortages, all fish items are doubled in price.
  • Cooking implements

Herbalist

The herbalist sells all healing items apart from faerian items and drugs. You can also request the herbalist patch you up for 2g per health point.

Houndmaster

The houndmaster, Ulot Ragnar, sells dogs and dog armour. The party will need to prove they can take care of a dog, and can only buy 1 dog maximum.

Dogs
  • Guard Dogs
  • Herd Dogs
  • War Dogs
Equipment

The houndmaster sells all available dog armour.

Library

You can visit the library to look up specific knowledge.

You can loan a book for 2 days for 50g.

  • Books can increase a relevant utility skill by 1 whilst in your inventory.
  • You can only loan 1 book at a time.
  • Failing to return your book results in a further 5g late fee per day.

Postmaster

You can visit the postmaster to deliver or receive messages. It costs 5g to deliver a message, and an additional 5g to have the postmaster write/read it if you are not literate.

Shipmaster

The current shipmaster, Sumi, is missing. His apprentice, Khaligudar is standing in.

There is only one ship available, a small nimble craft that can carry ~8 people called the "Blue Jay". Khaligudar will sell it for 5000g

Tailor

The local tailor sells all Clothing, Cloaks and Hats.

They also sell:

  • Tailoring tools

Blackmarket

The blackmarket is not readily accessible. You will need to get in their good graces first.

The blackmarket sells:

  • Drugs
  • Poisonous stuff
  • Sulfur
  • Lockpicking tools
  • Potion brewing equipment
  • Moonshining equipment
  • Numerian fire-starter
