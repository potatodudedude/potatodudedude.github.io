Environment and Terrain

Certain environmental factors will influence combat.

Obstacles

Obstacles block movement and cannot be stood on. They function as cover and can obstruct attacks.

High/Low Ground

Refer to ranged attack height advantages and disadvantages for bonuses/maluses to ranged attacks when regarding ranged attacks.

Levels

Difference in height between terrain is determined with an arbitrary "level" unit, representing roughly 1m difference in height.

Navigating onto higher ground under duress requires a jump action. Each level requires 2m of jumping distance and a DC10 dexterity check to reach. Alternatively, you can climb, which uses 2m of regular movement but requires a regular action per level.

When attempting to use melee attacks at someone higher/lower than you, the weapon range takes height level into account. For instance, someone can use a 2m range melee attack on someone directly adjacent if they are on terrain 1 level higher or lower.

  • Ranged attacks are also affected by this, but only when shooting from low ground to high ground.

Fall Damage

You can safely fall 2 levels before incurring damage. From the 3rd level on:

  • Take 1d6 blunt force trauma for every level you fall.

Difficult Terrain

Difficult terrain represents terrain that is hard to navigate, such as thick mud or slippery floors.

Moving across difficult terrain costs twice the movement points. Jumping is not affected.

Water

Water prevents you from swinging your weapons properly. When swimming, you can only grapple or use weapon moves that can be used during grappling. If afflicted with statuses like prone when swimming, you will be inflicted with drowning instead.

Swimming speed is determined by swimming skill.

  • If you have no skill, you cannot swim and will start drowning.
  • With 1 skill, your swimming speed is 33% that of your normal movement.
  • With 2 skill, your swimming speed is 66% that of your normal movement.
  • With 3 skill, your swimming speed is the same as your normal movement.

You cannot jump when swimming.

Hazards

Certain items like fire bottles will leave a lingering area of effect. For such areas, you are afflicted with the effect when entering the area and when you end your turn on them. The affliction on entering the area is limited to once a turn, so you cannot proc the effect over and over by leaving and entering the area again and again. Likewise, if you enter and end your turn in the effect on the same turn, the effect is only applied once on entering.

Lighting

Debuffs inflicted when attacking in the dark are only applied when the target is in the dark, not the attacker.

When attacking a target in the dark, all ranged weapons have their optimal range halved. In addition, hit chance is at disadvantage by 4 for all attacks.

Night Vision skill extends your vision in the dark by 1-3m, starting at 1m when at skill level 1 and increasing by 1m with each skill level. Night vision extending buffs work additively. (For instance, with level 3 night vision you would be able to see everything within 3m as normal. Using a torch with a 2m lighting radius, you would be able to see up to 2 + 3 = 5m.)
