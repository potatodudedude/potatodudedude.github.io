Traits

Traits are special buffs your character obtains as they level up.

Racial Traits

Cold Blooded:

Roll with disadvantage against hyperthermia and hypothermia.
Lose 1 to all your physical core stats on the 4th round of combat and beyond.

Books of History:
  • Gain +1 major/minor skill, or add 1 to an existing major/minor skill during character creation.
  • Gain +1 additional skill point each level up.
Godwin's Legacy:

Deal +1 blunt force trauma with all melee weapon attacks.

Sidewinder:

Deal +2 damage when flanking.

Tier 1

Armour Conditioning:

Gain +2kg to your equipment load thresholds.

Battle Nurse:

Allow you to use regular action medical items with a bonus action.

Big Drinker:

Level 2 drunkenness now doubles the effect of level 1 drunkenness. Level 3 drunkenness now makes enemy success against you always have complication.

Chaos Take Me:

Deal +1 damage when above 8 stress or when insane. Your stress can never fall below half its maximum.
When rolling insanities, roll twice and choose whichever you want.

Efficient Worker

Gain +2 time-units during long rests.

Enduring Body:

Allow you to use endurance for all physical saving throws.

Fast adapter:

On missing an attack, gain +2 hit chance. This effect can stack and is removed upon landing a hit.

Flexible Physique:

For checks that can use strength or dexterity, use both at the same time.

Iron Gut:

Ignore minor food poisoning and treat major food poisoning as minor food poisoning. Allows you to eat up to 2 units of food during short rests for 1d4 healing per unit.

Momentum:

+1m movement speed and +1 charge mobility when charging.

My Mind Is My Own:

Deal -2 damage when above 8 stress or when insane. Lose 1 stress per long rest.

Pack Rat:

Gain +25kg to your carrying capacity.

Rapid Rotation:

Allows you to spend a bonus action to switch places with an ally within 1m without either of you triggering opportunity attacks. The other party must consent to switch.

Thorough Scavenger:

Gain back 100% of spent ammunition when searching the battlefield instead of 50%.

Ghost of a People (Aquatine Humans only):

Ignore the effects of up to 2 points of exhaustion. This includes "addiction" exhaustion.

Blessed is the Day, Haunted is the Night (Numerian Humans only):

Gain +1 hit chance during the day. Gain +1 melee defence during the night.

Lined Pockets (Wingardian Humans only):

For every 500g in your inventory, gain a charge of lined pocket up to a maximum of 3.

  • Each charge allows you to half an instance of incoming damage.
  • Charges replenish every combat.
Thick Scales (Crocodilian only):

Gain +1 armour.

Large Wings (Faery only):

Gain +1m movement speed.

Tier 2

Agile:
  • Unlocks certain special moves with some weapons. You still need the relevant proficiencies.
  • When wielding any weapon that adds dexterity to melee defence, add dexterity to ranged defence also.
  • Allows you to move 1m without triggering opportunity attacks after a enemy misses a melee attack/grapple against you. You must still be in the enemy's range at the end of this movement.
Bloodhound:
  • Inflict 1 additional stack whenever you inflict bleeding.
  • When inflicting bleeding, instead of bleed being completely negated by a target with >0 effective armour, each point of effective armour only removes 1 stack of bleed.
Close Coordinator:

Remove disadvantage for hitting obstacles if you are directly adjacent to the obstacle. This allows you attack from directly behind your frontliner without risking hitting them.
In addition, allies are no longer considered obstacles when charging or lunging.

Critical Exploiter:

Allows you to get critical hits on a 19 as well. Only affects attack rolls.

Dual Wielder:

Allows you to dual wield all one-handed weapons without penalty. +1 hit chance when dual-wielding without shields.

False dragon:

With a bottle of coating, use a regular action to spit out the contents in an area, afflicting enemies with the coating's effects. Neurotoxins cannot be used this way.

Far Sighted:

Extend your ranged attacks' optimal range by 1m.

Gourmet:

Grant your team temporary hit points equal to double your cooking skill when long resting. When communal cooking, double your cooking skill for the cooking check. In addition, cooking recipes only require 0.5 time-units to make.

I don't care:

Always succeed on one saving throw of your choice per combat.

Lucky:

If you get a natural one, roll again. This can only happen once per roll.

Quick Hands:

Allows you to manage items and pick up items using bonus actions.

Shrug it off:

Negative effects lose 1 stack when inflicted on you. The stacks cannot be reduced below 1.

Sixth Sense:

Keep the defence bonuses from your weapons when flanked.

Thick Skin:

Reduce incoming blunt force trauma by your endurance modifier.

Too Angry to Die:

Grant your party buffs depending on the number of permanent injuries.

  • 1 permanent injury: +1 hit chance to allies within 1m.
  • 2 permanent injuries: +2 hit chance to allies within 2m.
  • 3 or more permanent injuries: +2 hit chance and +1 damage to allies within 3m.
    Effects are not cumulative.
Tough:

Gain an additional +1 max health per level. Applies retroactively.

Legions of the East (Aquatine Humans only):

Half the weight of weapons when in your equipment load.

Shepherds of the Ruined (Numerian Humans only):

Whenever you heal an ally, you recover the same amount of health.

Violence of Action (Wingardian Humans only):

On successful stealth ambushes, your party takes all their turns before the enemy takes a single turn for the first round, ignoring the zig-zag cycle.

Tsunami (Crocodilian only):

Gain +1 to all physical core stats when below half health.

Fury of the First (Faery only):

Roll initiative with advantage. Attack with advantage on your first turn.

Tier 3

Blood Frenzy:

Regain a regular action on a killing blow.

Butcher:

Inflict +50% damage under the following conditions:

  • Using a weapon with 0 base penetration.
    • Refers to before weapon customisations.
  • Attacking a target with 0 effective armour.
Deadly Flanker:

Allows you to execute combo finishers on your allies' combo starters on flanked enemies. Flanking attacks always hit.

Grappling Master:

Use expert level DC on all your grappling checks, and double your strength/dexterity modifier on saving throws involving grappling. On moves that already have expert level DC, enemies have disadvantage on their saving throws as well.

Immovable:

Roll your saving throws with advantage against attacks that move you.

Master Worker:

+4 time-units during long rests.

Peripheral Vision:

Widen your view cone by 45 degrees.

Savage Attacker:

Gain advantage on your damage rolls. Does not influence hit chance.
Mutually exclusive with Stalwart Defender.

Stalwart Defender

Enemy attacks against you have disadvantage on their damage rolls. Does not influence hit chance.
Mutually exclusive with Savage Attacker.

Unmake Me Yourself (Aquatine Humans only):

Once per long rest, survive a single fatal blow on 1 health.
Only direct weapon attacks can reduce your health to 0. All other effects (poison, bleeding, area of effect weapons) can only reduce your health to 1.

Godwin's Chosen (Numerian Humans only):

Double the effects of Godwin's Legacy.

Buried Viper (Wingardian Humans only):

Allows you to move without triggering an enemy's opportunity attacks if an ally is also in the view cone of the enemy.

Get Lost (Crocodilian only):

Use expert level DC on all weapon moves that move your enemies. On moves that already have expert level DC, enemies have disadvantage on their saving throws as well.

Effervescence (Faery only):

Allow passive effects from your pixie's masteries to spread to your party members within 1m. Does not include skill gains from masteries.

Non-player Traits

Weasley Miscreant:

Opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage if you're under light equipment load.

Shadow of a Titan:

During each round of combat, this combatant has turns equal to the number of players in combat. They can only take 1 turn per zig-zag cycle.
