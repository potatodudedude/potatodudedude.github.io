Statuses

Timed statuses lose 1 stack at the end of your turn, after the related effect happens.

Saving Throws:

Some statuses can be avoided using a saving throw against a difficulty class (DC for short).

Saving throw calculation is given by a d20 + relevant core stat. If your saving throw is greater than the difficulty class, you avoid the status.

Saving Throw DC Calculations

Some statuses, such as those used by coatings, have a fixed DC value declared on their wiki page. Other statuses may have no DC value declared, or simply declare "Expert DC". For these statuses, use the below calculations:

For statuses inflicted by weapons:

  • Normal saving throw DC is given by 10 + relevant core stat of attacker + attacker weapon proficiency.
  • Expert saving throw DC is given by 15 + relevant core stat of attacker + attacker weapon proficiency.
    For other statuses:
  • Normal saving throw DC is given by 10 + relevant core stat of attacker + attacker veterancy.
  • Expert saving throw DC is given by 15 + relevant core stat of attacker + attacker veterancy.

The relevant core stat of the attacker is usually the same as the stat that provides the hit chance bonus of the weapon.

Effect Areas

When dealing with an area of effect, such as a pool of fire/cloud of poison, you suffer their relevant effect when entering and when ending your turn in the area.
This is limited to once a turn, so repeatedly entering and leaving an area of effect on the same turn will not trigger its effects multiple times. Likewise, entering and ending you turn in the effect on the same turn will only see the effect applied once on entering.

Addicted:

Endurance saving throw.

  • If you don't take the relevant substance at least once per long rest, you gain 2 points of "addiction' exhaustion, which functions the same as regular exhaustion and stacks with it but cannot be removed through long resting unlike normal exhaustion.
    • This "addiction" exhaustion is only removed by taking the relevant substance or by clearing the addiction.
  • Taking the related substance will cancel the effects of addiction until the next long rest.
  • Going two long rests without taking the relevant substance will clear the addiction.
  • Saves start at DC10 and increases by 5 every time you use a substance over its daily safe amount.
    • This resets on long rest.

Bleeding:

Endurance saving throw.

  • If a weapon attack's penetration is not greater than the target's armour (i.e. effective armour > 0), the status is not applied.
    • The exception is with the bloodhound trait, which has the bleed status lose 1 stack for every point of effective armour instead of being removed outright.
  • Take 1 direct damage at the end of your turn.
  • On reaching 6 or more stacks, lose 6 stacks and take 10 direct damage or half your health as direct damage immediately, depending on which is greater.

Blinded:

Endurance saving throw. Become unable to see, limiting your perception to just 2m, and imposing disadvantage on all attacks.
All attacks against you have advantage.

Broken Ankle:

Endurance saving throw. Half your movement speed, and you can no longer use any moves that move you.

Broken Wrist:

Endurance saving throw. Half your regular damage, penetration and blunt force trauma. Direct damage is not affected.

Burning:

Endurance saving throw. Take 1 direct damage per stack at the end of your turn.

  • Can be put out by using the free action or having someone else use the free action from you.
  • DC 10 + 1 for every stack.
  • A free action that fails will still remove 2 stacks.

Corroded:

Endurance saving throw. Lose 1 armour per stack active on you.

Dazed:

Endurance saving throw. You can now only do one type of action on your turn:

  • Move
  • Regular action
  • Bonus action

Dazzled:

Endurance saving throw. Attack with disadvantage, including opportunity and zoning attacks. Every attack made reduces the effect by 1 stack.

Deadly Poison:

Endurance saving throw. Take 4 direct damage at the end of your turn. Suffer -3 to your hit chance and defenses for the duration of the poison.

Disarmed:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. Drop your weapon on the same tile.

Drunk:

Endurance saving throw. Being drunk has stages, lasting an hour each:

  • Level 1: -1 to core mental stats, Dexterity and hit chance, +1 to Strength.
  • Level 2: All successes are rolled with complication.
  • Level 3: Black out, suffer the same effects of minor food poisoning.
    Effects are cumulative.
    When the timer for 1 level expires, the timer for the previous level begins.
    With the big drinker trait:
  • Effects of level 1 are doubled.
  • Level 3 effects are replaced with: All enemy successes against you are rolled with complication.
  • Level 3 with big drinker only lasts 1 turn.

Drowning:

Endurance saving throw. Starts at DC10, increasing by 2 for every consecutive check.
Failing the check will make you take 20% of your max health as direct damage.

Exhaustion:

For every exhaustion point:

  • Suffer -1 to all core stats, melee defense and ranged defense.
    In addition, from the third exhaustion point and onwards:
  • -5 or 25% max health, whichever is higher.
    • This can kill you if it reduces your max health to 0.

Food Poisoning:

Endurance saving throw.
Minor: Default DC 12 save. Your max health is reduced by 25% until your next long rest, -1 endurance.
Major: Default DC 20 save. Your max health is reduced by 50% until your next long rest, -2 endurance.
You may suffer stomach aches/vomiting at the DM's convenience.

Grappled:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. Both grappler and target are unable to move or use most weapon moves, and lose the defence bonuses from their weapons. Grappling is removed when:

  • One party uses a free action or has the free action used on them.
  • One party is moved by an outside force.
    Whilst grappled, both sides must have the other as a view cone restriction.
    Bear in mind the initiator cannot leave the grapple freely. They must succeed in the free action or have the opponent agree to let them go.

Hyperthermia:

Endurance saving throw.
When in an extremely hot environment, roll a hyperthermia check every hour. Failing it will result in:

  • -1m movement speed.
  • -1 to all physical core stats.
  • 1d4 direct damage taken.
    For every level of equipment load threshold above light, suffer -5 to your saving throw.

Hypothermia:

Endurance saving throw.
When in an extremely cold environment, roll a hypothermia check every hour. Failing it will result in:

  • -1m movement speed.
  • -1 to all physical core stats.
  • 1d4 direct damage taken.
    For every level of equipment load threshold below heavy, suffer -5 to your saving throw.

Invisible:

Cannot be seen. All attacks count as flanking attacks. Enemies cannot shift their view cone to defend your attack.
Enemies that know your general location can attempt to hit you with disadvantage.
Invisibility is broken on performing any regular action or attack.
Invisibility is broken on being damaged.

Marander Gas:

DC15 endurance saving throw. Crocodilians are immune to this status.
Suffer -2 to hit chance and defenses, alongside taking 1d4 direct damage.

Metal Plague:

Endurance saving throw. If you are wearing metal armour, suffer the following:

  • Half your movement speed.
  • Disadvantage on attacks, and attacks against you have advantage.
  • Half your armour.

Move:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. Get moved a specified distance and direction. Being moved usually does not trigger opportunity attacks, but can trigger zoning attacks.
If you are moved into a space that's already occupied, you take 1d4 blunt force trauma instead. If a character is the one occupying the space, they take the same blunt force trauma.

Neurotoxin:

No saving throw. Neurotoxin has several properties:

  • Take 20% of your health or 5 direct damage for each stack at the end of your turn, depending on which is higher.
  • The direct damage bypasses temporary health, as well as any gating effects, such as the unmake me yourself trait.
  • You cannot heal. Using healing items can still confer their other positive effects, but will not give you any health.
  • Neurotoxin stacks do not decay and cannot be reduced by bonuses/traits/paths that reduce the stack count of negative effects.
  • Neurotoxin cannot be cured and will result in death. The only exception is if it is stopped with a revelation of neutrality.

Numerian Fire:

No saving throw. Numerian fire has several effects:

  • At the end of your turn, take 1 direct damage per stack.
  • Lose armour equivalent to the number of stacks.
  • Numerian fire doubles in stack at the end of the turn instead of decaying.
  • Once Numerian fire has enough stacks to nullify all your armour, you can make a DC20 Strength | Dexterity check to remove all body armour, removing all stacks of fire.
  • If used on the environment, numerian fire spreads to a maximum of 10m.

Pinned:

No saving throw. Become pinned, which:

  • Removes your ability to dash.
  • Any weapon move that allows you to move whilst ignoring opportunity attacks now only gives incoming opportunity attacks disadvantage.

Poison:

Endurance saving throw. Take 2 direct damage at the end of your turn. Suffer -2 to your hit chance and defenses for the duration of the poison.

Prone:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. Being prone has many properties:

  • If on your turn, lose 3/4 of your max movement speed.
  • Movement speed is capped at 1/4.
  • Attacks against you count as flanking attacks.
  • Suffer disadvantage on all your attacks.
  • You can only use moves with a range of 1m. You cannot use moves that move you.
  • This status lasts until you use a free action or someone uses the free action on you, Dexterity DC10.
  • Failing the free action to get up will result in the removal of this condition anyway, but the next attack against you still counts as a flanking attack.

Run Over:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. Take the horse's built up charge force as blunt force trauma. Regardless of saving throw success, you cannot execute opportunity or zoning attacks against the thing running you over for the duration of the charge.

  • On successful save: Take half the runover blunt force trauma and stop the horse's charge, reflecting the full runover blunt force trauma onto the horse.
  • On failure: Fall Prone and get sent flying back 1m for every 3 charge force the horse has built up. If you are moved into a space that's already occupied, you stop short and take 1d4 blunt force trauma. If a character is the one occupying the space, they take the same blunt force trauma.

Shellshock:

Endurance saving throw. Suffer -2 to your defences and -2 to hit chance.

Splintered Shield:

Endurance saving throw. Lose all bonuses from your shield, cancel all shield moves in effect, and prevent you from using any shield moves until your next turn. Bucklers are not affected.

Stardust:

No saving throw. Nebulans are immune.
Stardust deals 5 direct damage or 25% of your max health, whichever is higher.

Strangled:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. Take direct damage equal to the attacker's strength modifier at the end of their turn. Your vision cone is forced to face directly away from the attacker. The condition is cancelled when you make a successful grappling move against the attacker or if the grapple is removed.

Suppressed:

Endurance saving throw. Gain disadvantage on ranged attacks and half movement speed.

Tangled:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. You cannot move or use weapon moves that move you, and suffer -2 to your attacks and defences. Status ends once you use a free action or someone uses the free action on you.

Tangled Weapon:

Strength | Dexterity saving throw. Your weapon is restricted by something. You cannot use weapon moves. Tangled weapon is removed when:

  • You use a free action or has the free action used on you.
  • You drop the weapon.

Unknowable:

Cannot be seen. All attacks count as flanking attacks. Enemies cannot shift their view cone to defend against your attacks.
Enemies that know your general location can attempt to hit you with disadvantage.
This form of invisibility can only be broken with a revelation of neutrality.

Insanity

You are subject to some kind of insanity. These last for 2 long rests or a duration at the GM's discretion.

  1. Hyper-fixation: In combat, you become hyper-fixated on a random target, becoming unable to attack anyone other than them. Once your target is down, you hyper-fixate on a new target.
  2. Ramblings: You ramble incoherently, making stealth impossible and gaining disadvantage in social interactions.
  3. Penitent Silence: You become voluntarily mute.
  4. Cowardice: You will never voluntarily step into range of a enemy melee weapon.
  5. Uncoordinated Speech: You can only say the opposite of what you mean.
  6. Uncoordinated Listening: You understand the opposite of anything you hear.
  7. Uncoordinated in General: Any targeted action has a 50% chance to be redirected onto you.
  8. Hallucinations: You gain a new imaginary friend. Or foe :0.
  9. Substance Abuse: Anytime you come into contact with any alcohol/drugs, you have to take them.
  10. Selective Blindness: You become unable to see a type of thing or person.
  11. Identity Theft: Adopt the mannerisms, philosophy and dress of someone else.
  12. Lucky Charm: Form an obsession with some inconvenient object, suffering disadvantage to everything if not within 5m of it.
  13. Unlucky Charm: Form an obsession with some inconvenient object, suffering disadvantage to everything if within 5m of it.
  14. Masochist: Your melee defense is set to 0.
  15. Gambler: Base all decisions on a dice roll.
  16. Naturalist: Refuse to use any metallic item.
  17. Unnaturalist: Refuse to use any non-metallic item.
  18. Duck: Become only able to say and write the word "Duck".
  19. Placebo Remodeling: Swap your lowest and highest core stats.
  20. Glare back into the Abyss: Heal up to half your health, and all other party members lose 2 stress.
