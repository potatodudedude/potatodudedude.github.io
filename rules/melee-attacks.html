Melee Attacks

I've tried to make melee combat feel more immersive and strategic, so now there are several new mechanics tied to melee combat. Bear in mind that combat maps are separate into 1m by 1m grids to calculate distance.

Move sets:

Each weapon type now has a unique set of moves. Moves can be unlocked by investing proficiency points during level ups.

Range:

Each melee weapon now has a default optimum range. This represents the range the weapon is usually most effective at. Short weapons like one-handed swords, maces, etc. now usually have a range of 1m. Long weapons like pikes, polearms etc. now have a range of 2-3m. Note that this range is move-specific, so a weapon can have multiple optimum ranges besides their default one.

Obstructed Attack:

For attacks with >1m range, if there is an obstruction between you and the target, you roll with disadvantage, the same as to obstructions used by ranged attacks.

View Cones and Flanking Attacks:

A character has a zone of attention when attacking and defending. Imagine your character has a view cone extending from them with a default FOV of 90 degrees.
Making an attack will automatically lock your view cone such that the target has to be within it. This applies to opportunity/zoning attacks.

In order to attack someone else, you must be able to shift your view cone to include the new target whilst still encompassing all old enemies

Upon being attacked by another enemy:

  • If you can shift your view cone to include the new enemy whilst still encompassing all old enemies, you defend as normal, but your view cone restrictions must now include the new enemy.
  • If you can't, the new enemy gets a flanking attack on you.

Flanking attacks grant advantage, and remove the defence bonuses of the target's weapons.

You may choose to deliberately avoid defending attacks so that your view cone isn't as restricted, allowing you to defend against potentially more dangerous enemies that might flank you.

View cone restrictions are reset at the start of your next turn. They can also be lifted for specific enemies depending on the situation.

View cone stretching

Upon leaving melee range of the enemy:

  • If you are both out of range, the view cone restrictions for both of you are lifted.
  • If you are in range but the enemy isn't, you may chose to break your restrictions, but doing so will cause the enemy's to be lifted as well.
  • If the enemy is in range but you aren't, your restrictions are only lifted if the enemy decides to.
  • This does not apply to ranged attacks. Those restrictions can only be removed at the start of your next turn or by view cone cracking.
View cone cracking

In the case where an enemy already in your view cone restriction list manages to move in such a way that you are no longer able to encompass them and everyone else in the restriction list, they are removed from your view cone restriction list, and you are removed from theirs.

Opportunity Attacks:

If an enemy moves out of or within your weapon's default optimal range, you may attack them. Bear in mind this imposes and is subject to view cone restrictions until your next turn. Opportunity attacks don't trigger on entering your optimal range.

You only have 1 opportunity attack a turn.

Enemies hit by opportunity attacks have their movement cancelled, and all remaining movement speed removed. Enemies can still use their regular action to dash afterwards.

Zoning Attacks:

Some weapon moves allow you to gain a zoning buff, where you can attack enemies that enter your weapon's optimal range. Bear in mind this imposes and is subject to view cone restrictions until your next turn. Zoning attacks don't trigger on enemies leaving or moving within your weapon's optimal range.

You have unlimited zoning attacks until the buff wears off.

Unlike opportunity attacks, enemies retain their movement when hit by zoning attacks.

Charging

Charging on foot requires you to move in a straight line along one of the 8 directions on a square grid (+ and x).

  • For every 3m moved, add your strength | dexterity modifier as bonuses to your weapon's hit chance and regular damage.
  • The 3m intervals refer to distance moved on the ground, not movement spent.
  • Enemy opportunity and zoning attacks made against you during the charge will gain your charge bonuses against you.
  • You finish the charge from the enemy at the range your weapon's default attack is.
  • You cannot charge through obstacles, allies or enemies.

Charge Mobility

Each point of charge mobility allows you to make a 45 degree turn at any point during your charge.

Movement moves

Moves that try to move a target must hit, then the target must fail the move saving throw to succeed. The damage from the hit is still applied even if the target succeeds the saving throw.

Moves that allow the user and hit a target and move must hit for the user to be able to move without triggering opportunity attacks. The user may choose to not move if they miss. If they hit, they must move.
