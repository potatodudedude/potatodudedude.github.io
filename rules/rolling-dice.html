Rolling Dice

This system uses d20 for hit chance and skill checks, rolled against the difficulty class (DC) of a certain task. You succeed if your roll is greater than the DC.
Most of the time, you'll add a modifier to the roll depending on what relevant stats/skills you have.
All values are rounded up.

Complications

When rolling any check, if your roll matches the difficulty check exactly, you succeed at some cost. For attacks, whether the cost is a minor or major depends on proficiency. For everything else, it will usually be a major complication.

Major Complications

Attacks:
  1. The enemy gets in a counter attack.
    • If you are at ranged and the enemy cannot reach you, another ranged enemy will make the attack if possible.
    • If not, then reroll the complication.
  2. Your weapon/hand gets stuck.
    • The thing can be stuck in the enemy or ground, preventing you from using it and removing the melee defense bonuses from it.
    • Moving away/The enemy moving away will cause you to leave your weapon stuck in whatever it's in. In the case of martial arts, this will cause your next attack to be at disadvantage/enemies to have advantage against your next grappling attempt.
    • Requires a free action, DC12. Failing the action will still allow you to free your weapon, but your next attack will be at disadvantage.
    • Free action can be done by someone else.
  3. Your weapon glances, doing half damage, pen and BFT.
  4. You fumble and get dazed(1), no save.
  5. Your foot gets stuck.
    • You become unable to move, including weapon moves that move you.
    • Requires a free action, DC12. Failing the action will still allow you to free your foot, but your next attack will be at disadvantage.
    • Free action can be done by someone else.
  6. You expose yourself, the next attack against you has advantage/you have disadvantage on your next grappling saving throw until your next turn.
  7. You accidentally hit yourself for half the effect too.
    • If the effect cannot be halved, then you take the full brunt of the action.
  8. You drop your weapon. If unarmed, drop something else in your equipment load at random.
Generic:

Subject to change based on situation.

  1. Your tool/item gets destroyed/disabled temporarily.
  2. You make a bunch of noise.
  3. You trip and fall on your ass.
  4. You twist something and gain disadvantage on some skill/stat.

Minor Complications

Attacks:
  1. The enemy gets in a counter attack at disadvantage.
    • If you are at ranged and the enemy cannot reach you, another ranged enemy will make the attack if possible.
    • If not, then reroll the complication.
  2. Your weapon/hand gets slightly stuck.
    • The thing can be stuck in the enemy or ground, preventing you from using it and removing the melee defense bonuses from it.
    • Moving away/The enemy moving away will cause your weapon to be free, but your next attack will have disadvantage. In case of martial arts, the enemy will have advantage on saves against your next grappling attempt.
    • Requires a free action, DC0.
    • Free action can be done by someone else.
    • If stuck on an enemy, you can also free yourself by using a move that moves you/the enemy.
  3. Your foot gets slightly stuck.
    • You become unable to move, including weapon moves that move you.
    • Requires a free action, DC0.
    • Free action can be done by someone else.
  4. You fumble and get dazed(1), DC10.

Critical Successes and Failures

Natural Ones

When you roll a natural 1, you always fail the roll regardless of any modifiers or the DC. In addition, you suffer some form of catastrophic consequence.

Natural Twenties

When you roll a natural 20, you always succeed the roll regardless of how high the DC is. In addition, you gain some tremendous benefit.

Critical Hits

If you score a critical hit on an attack you:

  • Double your regular damage, penetration and blunt force trauma.
  • Any damage rolls use their maximum value.

Inspirations

Exceptional events are awarded with inspiration points. Inspiration points can be spent to reroll any roll you make, and you can have a maximum of 3 points.

Inspiration is awarded when doing things like being creative, engaging in roleplay etc.
