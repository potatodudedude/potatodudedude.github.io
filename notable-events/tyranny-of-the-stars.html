Tyranny of the Stars

The night of 21st July 1214 marks the start of the Nebulan invasion of the continent that would change it forever. The following is a timeline of events during the invasion, now termed "The tyranny of the Stars". The Nebulans sent about 250000 soldiers, embarked upon over 1000 warships. A race of lanky, purple-skinned humanoids, the Nebulans wield strange, glowing crystal weapons and secrete Stardust. Stardust is a shimmering like mist with terrifying properties, killing any non-Nebulan creature within seconds. It cannot be displaced by wind or be contained by regular containers. Upon first contact, the people of the continent had no way to fight against it.

Thanks to information sent by The Canaries, the general war plan of the Nebulans was made known before their invasion. First, the Nebulans aimed to destroy the Aquatine Empire first, eliminating the largest army on the continent and crippling food supply. Simultaneously, they aimed to destroy the Conclave of the Steel Faeries, for fear that the pixies may engineer a way to counter Stardust.

Attack of The Right Claw

The very first settlement to come under attack, a fleet of almost 500 ships landed near the north-eastern shore of the continent. Having be forewarned by The Canaries, Captain Li Ma, head of the local guard had organised a proper defence, evacuating civilians, preparing food stockpiles, fortifications and scattering debris in the sea surrounding the claw to impede the incoming ships. With the Aquatine Empire's second legion already garrisoned there due to the Insurrection at the Claw, the defenders were able to hold out, attempting to delay melee combat for as long as possible. However, the rest of the empire did not heed The Canaries or Captain Li Ma's warnings. The attack commenced on the night of 21st July 1214, but by the morning, the Claw was the only place on the north-eastern shores not covered in shimmering mist.

Attack of The Citadel

Months before the invasion, various ships had been disappearing off the northern coastline. Shortly after the invasion of the Claw, the missing ships arrive at the Citadel in an attempt to launch a Trojan horse attack. Having received warning from The Canaries, the Citadel's heavy cannons fire on the fleet before they have the chance to get close. Following this failure, the rest of the Nebulan fleet began a protracted siege. The Citadel's defences prove formidable, and the Nebulans would be stuck attempting to breach its walls for the remainder of the war. Forces from the Kingdom of Numeria attempted to help, but were no match for the Nebulan's Stardust.

Invasion of the Aquatine Empire

As news of the Nebulan threat sweeps through the empire, a mass panic began. Millions of citizens flooded south, creating a massive humanitarian crisis. The Imperial legions, once the hallmark of human military might, find themselves powerless against the Nebulans' Stardust. The empire attempts to adapt away from their conventional tactics, focusing on defensive forts and horse archers to better counter this new threat. Whilst these measures help, they are nowhere near enough to turn the tide. By 19th August 1214, almost all of the Empire's lands have fallen. Only The Right Claw and the mighty walls of the capital, Stonebasin, continue to hold out. But with food reserves slowly dwindling and the survivable area of each settlement being chipped away each day as stardust is catapulted over the walls, their days are numbered. Emperor Marcus Callier the First is slain during the defense of Stonebasin, alongside his heir, princess Mikayla Callier.

Invasion of the Serpent Archipelagos and the Wingard Desert

After the Aquatine Empire has all but shattered, the Nebulans turn their attention to the other factions. Curiously, the Kingdom of Numeria remains untouched. As the Nebulans invade the Crocodilian islands, they find them already evacuated, with only a few berserkers of the Steelscale Clan remaining to test their mettle against this new threat. They last impressively long within the stardust, ripping and tearing, but eventually they all fall. When the Nebulan fleet attempts to invade Snake's Throat, the smaller but more agile Wingardian navy conducts harassing attacks to disrupt them. On land, the Wingard Collective evacuate their people and deny the Nebulans a pitched engagement, making use of their familiarity of their home terrain to delay them in skirmishes. Despite this, the Nebulans eventually start claiming the southern coastlines, and force the Wingardians further inland, towards Numerian land.

Invasion of Sonostra

As the Aquatine Empire crumbles and the Nebulan fleet begins moving down the eastern coast, the wealthy elites of Sonostra promptly abandon their city, fleeing with their mercenary soldiers to the mainland. The city is left in disarray, and most people try to flee as well. But with most ships being sent to war, many are left stranded, too poor to bribe their way aboard the few available vessels. Many die attempting to swim to the mainland, and many more die when the stardust descends upon the city.

Revelation of the Stars

On 3rd September 1214, news comes from the scientists of the Citadel: Thanks to the sample of stardust sent in advance by The Canaries, a counter has been engineered to stardust. A chemical process can be done quickly by the Pixies to convert Stardust into Marander Gas. Marander Gas is still toxic to most living beings, but Crocodilians are immune to it, and protective breathing apparatus can be made to filter it, unlike Stardust. With this knowledge, a coalition is rapidly formed amongst the surviving factions. The Faeries and Pixies convert Stardust into Marander Gas, whilst the crocodilians and humans with hastily made breathing equipment act as frontline soldiers. This new coalition army launches a series of counter attacks against the Nebulans, and see much initial success. The Nebulan's stardust is turned against them, and without protection of their own against the Marander gas, they are rapidly driven back by the coalition. The situation is not all good news, as while Marander Gas can be broken down into safe components, the process is long and laborious, leaving most of the conquered lands still uninhabitable.

Revenge of the Titan

On 15th December 1214, the last of the Nebulans are chased off the continent. The Right Claw, Stonebasin and The Citadel are relieved of their sieges, and the coalition begins to plan their next move. All the surviving leaders agree on one thing: The Nebulans cannot be allowed to return. With the Wingardian and Numerian navy still somewhat intact, as well as numerous captured Nebulan ships, the coalition form an expeditionary force, The Last Legion. With the Aquatine lands covered in toxic Marander Gas, the continent suffers a massive food shortage. The Nebulans brought with them a fungus, capable of surviving Stardust and growing off of corpses. With no shortage of bodies following the devastation, the coalition resorts to this grim source of sustenance for their expeditionary army.

As the Last Legion chases the Nebulans back North, they pass the Isle of Andromeda, discovering the remains of The Canaries and Shadows of the Nebulans. Upon sailing further north, they come across the home continent of the Nebulans, the Eye of the Storm. With long nights and an extensive underground cave system, the Last Legion endures on their arduous task with grim determination: The genocide of the Nebulan species. Conditions are horrible, and the Last Legion endures starvation, disease and atrocity alike. After 5 long years, their duty is complete.

Aftermath

Although the Nebulans were defeated, the continent is ruined. The once fertile lands of the Aquatine Empire are no more, alongside the empire itself. Of the 60 million Aquatine humans, only 15 million survive, with more dying everyday to starvation and disease. Whilst the second legion of the empire manage to survive by holding out in The Right Claw, most chose to join The Last Legion to seek revenge. The only semblance of governance and military of the Aquatine Empire comes from the few ruling mayors and generals who managed to survive in Stonebasin. Now, the Aquatines are in a dire sorry state, living as refugees in the Numerian and Wingardian lands, a far cry from their dominance before the war.

The Numerians were the least affected, with 24 out of 30 million surviving. Although the Numerians claim not to know why the Nebulans avoided targeting them, many accuse them of collaborating with the Nebulans, especially after the actions of the Shadows of the Nebulans come to light, where New Illuminetres fanatics seemingly carried out the bidding of the Nebulans to prepare for their invasion. Despite this, the Numerian people still suffer greatly, with many dying of famine and the overcrowding of refugees in their lands.

The Wingardians managed to mitigate their losses somewhat by denying the Nebulans a direct confrontation, but still end up with only 7 out of 10 million of their population surviving. Quick to adapt, they set up new settlements in the desert, but ultimately still feel the massive manpower drain and severe food shortage.

The Conclave of the steel faeries saw half of their population of 1.5 million faeries and pixies dead, with most dying defending the Citadel or fighting as part of the coalition army. The Citadel remains largely intact, and faery society is still somewhat stable. But, with a severe pixie shortage after the war, they are left with limited ability to cleanse the land of Marander Gas or leverage their technology for rebuilding.

The Crocodilians see only 3 million of their original 7 million population surviving. Being immune to Marander Gas, the coalition relied heavily on them as soldiers for the war, with many dying fighting the Nebulans and more sent to pursue them as part of the Last Legion. Thanks to their immunity to Marander Gas, the crocodilians are the few who avoid the overcrowding of refugees by settling back in their original islands and parts of the now destroyed Aquatine Empire. However, they are still heavily affected by the food crisis.

The continent will never be the same again, and with the common enemy gone, the factions begin to turn on each other again. Accusations are leveraged against the Numerians for cooperating with the invaders, people lament the faeries for not doing more to cleanse the Marander Gas, and hateful rhetoric is directed at the refugees of the Aquatine Empire for taking up valuable resources. It will likely take centuries, maybe even millennia before things can even start to go back to the way it was...
