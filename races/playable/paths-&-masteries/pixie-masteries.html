Pixie Masteries

Pixie traits work differently than normal traits. When a pixie gets to choose a trait, they may choose to evolve any one mastery. Evolving the same mastery multiple times will unlock higher tier effects. Tier effects do not stack with previous tiers.

At the final tier of mastery, you can spend a focus point to perform a pixie revelation. Revelations can be activated at anytime without an action, even during the enemy's turn.

Only 1 offensive mastery and 1 utility mastery can be active at any 1 time. This does not apply to the skill gain from the masteries.

Skill totals cannot go above 3.

If not stated otherwise, all effects use the normal saving throw DC of 10 + your pixie's intelligence, which is usually 5 + your veterancy.

Offensive Masteries

Acid Mastery:

The acute study of materials has also yielded acute ways to destroy them. Pixie chemists studying acid are able to create finely tuned particles that weaken or dissolve enemy armour whilst leaving the blades of their allies intact.

Tier 1:

  • Add +1 penetration to your weapon attacks.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect for 1 turn.
  • Gain +1 chemistry(acid) skill.

Tier 2:

  • Add +2 penetration to your weapon attacks.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect for 2 turns.
  • Gain +2 chemistry(acid) skill.

Tier 3:

  • Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of Null.
  • Add +2 penetration to your weapon attacks.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect for 3 turns.
  • Gain +3 chemistry(acid) skill.

Revelation of Null:

The peak of acid engineering comes in the null acid, able to dissolve through metal as though it wasn't there to begin with.

  • Coat your weapon with a shimmering mist of null acid, allowing your next successful attack to:
    • Ignore armour.
    • Ignore the melee defense bonuses from the target's weapons.
    • Damage the target's weapon(s) beyond use.
  • If an attack would successfully hit you, you may choose to damage the incoming weapon beyond use to nullify the attack, removing the coating in the process.
  • The coating is limited to 1 use.
  • The coating is not visible without a perception check.
  • The coating lasts for 1 hour.

Fire Mastery:

The oldest experiment in the world, fire-making, still bears relevance as the ages advance. An essential part of life often taken for granted, pixies harness the use of fire for more than just cooking or warmth.

Tier 1:

  • Inflict burning(1) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect and make the DC expert level for 1 turn.
  • Gain +1 fire making skill.

Tier 2:

  • Inflict burning(2) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect and make the DC expert level for 2 turns.
  • Gain +2 fire making skill.

Tier 3:

  • Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of Ashes.
  • Inflict burning(2) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect and make the DC expert level for 3 turns.
  • Gain +3 fire making skill.

Revelation of Ashes:

Numerian fire is greenish-blue, developed to counter the heavily armoured human warriors during The War of the Meadows. Once ignited, it cannot be stopped.

  • Coat your weapon with a shimmering mist of potent fuel, allowing your next successful attack to ignite and inflict Numerian fire(2).
  • The coating is limited to 1 use.
  • The coating is not visible without a perception check.
  • The coating lasts for 1 hour.

Poison Mastery:

The art of poison making is one that is ever evolving, and is also a controversial aspect of pixie experimentation. Many in the ruling council cite concerns over ethics and legality, but behind closed doors, the work continues.

Tier 1:

  • Inflict poison(1) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • You can spend a focus point to inflict deadly poison(1) and make the DC expert level for 1 turn.
  • Gain +1 chemistry(poison) skill.

Tier 2:

  • Inflict poison(2) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • You can spend a focus point to inflict deadly poison(2) and make the DC expert level for 2 turns.
  • Gain +2 chemistry(poison) skill.

Tier 3:

Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of Death.

  • Inflict poison(2) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • You can spend a focus point to inflict deadly poison(2) and make the DC expert level for 3 turns.
  • Gain +3 chemistry(poison) skill.

Revelation of Death:

Neurotoxin is the magnum opus of pixie poisoners. Causing brain death in moments, the only hope for one afflicted by neurotoxin is the intervention of a master pixie.

  • Coat your weapon with a shimmering mist of engineered neurotoxin, allowing your next successful attack to inflict neurotoxin(1).
  • If an attack's regular damage is completely negated by armour, the revelation will not activate.
  • The coating is limited to 1 use.
  • The coating is not visible without a perception check.
  • The coating lasts for 1 hour.

Powder Mastery:

Gunpowder is a relatively new invention amongst pixies, brought about after the formation of the Conclave of the Steel Faeries. Such is the power of gunpowder that many other factions try to create their own, but never to the standards that pixies are capable of.

Tier 1:

  • Inflict dazed(1) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect and make the DC expert level for 1 turn.
  • Gain +1 chemistry(powder) skill.

Tier 2:

  • Inflict dazed(1) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • Enemies within a 1m radius around the target are also affected.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect and make the DC expert level for 2 turns.
  • Gain +2 chemistry(powder) skill.

Tier 3:

Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of Thunder.

  • Inflict dazed(1) on hit.
    • Limited to once a turn.
    • Enemies within a 1m radius around the target are also affected.
    • You can spend a focus point to double the effect and make the DC expert level for 3 turns.
  • Gain +3 chemistry(powder) skill.

Revelation of Thunder:

With the invention of gunpowder, many faeries and pixies sought to create weapons and means to utilise it. Despite the advancement of grenades, cannons and muskets, sometimes the best way is to just use the powder straight up.

  • Your pixie spreads a fine dust of concentrated black powder amongst the enemy, allowing you to detonate up to 4 tiles within 3m of you, dealing 2d6 blunt force trauma to those tiles.
  • Inflicts shellshock(1), guaranteed.
  • The tiles do not need to be next to each other.

Utility Masteries

Counter Mastery:

With the increasing amount of creative ways pixies come up with to kill people came a sudden realisation amongst the leading council of faeries and pixies. And so, the school of counter mastery was born, seeking to put a lid on the growing power of the alchemists in case of a civil war.

Tier 1:

  • Lower all pixie effects within 2m by 1 tier.
    • You can spend a focus point to remove 1 effect within 3m.
      • This acts as a free action, using your pixie's intelligence on the d20 roll.
      • This cures any poison, deadly poison, burning, bleeding, dazed, broken ankles or broken wrists automatically.

Tier 2:

  • Lower all pixie effects within 2m by 2 tiers.
    • You can spend a focus point to gain 2 counter charges that negate effects within 3m.
      • Each charge lasts for 1 hour and can only negate 1 effect/count as 1 free action.
      • This acts as a free action, using your pixie's intelligence on the d20 roll.
      • This cures any poison, deadly poison, burning, bleeding, dazed, broken ankles or broken wrists automatically.

Tier 3:

  • Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of Neutrality.
  • Lower all pixie effects within 2m by 3 tiers.
    • You can spend a focus point to gain 3 counter charges that negate effects within 3m.
      • Each charge lasts for 1 hour and can only negate 1 effect/count as 1 free action.
      • This acts as a free action, using your pixie's intelligence on the d20 roll.
      • This cures any poison, deadly poison, burning, bleeding, dazed, broken ankles or broken wrists automatically.
  • Gain +1 to:
    • Chemistry(acid) skill.
    • Chemistry(gunpowder) skill.
    • Chemistry(poison) skill.
    • Fire making skill.

Revelation of Neutrality:

To destroy is easy; To build is difficult. This principle applies to how the revelations can wreak havoc in the blink of an eye, but also applies to these revelations themselves.

  • Your pixie enters a trance of active defense, allowing you to neutralise any other revelation within 2m.
  • The revelation has only 1 use.
  • The neutralisation can activate automatically when another revelation activates.
  • You may choose not to activate your revelation automatically.
  • The trance lasts for 1 hour.

Light Mastery:

This mastery is given for free.
The most basic form of alchemy taught to a pixie is the ability to create light. A simple and useful reaction, light can be harnessed to achieve incredible means.

Tier 1:

  • Spend a focus point to impose disadvantage on 1 attack against you or an ally within 3m.
    • Weapon moves that attack multiple times have the entire attack affected.
    • This is done through a bright flash of light.
  • Gain +1 ranged weapon optimal range.
  • Gain +1 night vision skill.

Tier 2:

  • Spend a focus point to gain 2 light charges which allow you to impose disadvantage on attacks against you or an ally within 3m.
    • This is done through a bright flash of light.
    • Each charge only affects 1 attack. Weapon moves that attack multiple times have the entire attack affected.
    • These light charges last for 1 hour.
  • Gain +1 ranged weapon optimal range.
  • Gain +2 night vision skill.

Tier 3:

  • Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of the Sun.
  • Spend a focus point to gain 3 light charges which allow you to impose disadvantage on attacks against you or an ally within 3m.
    • This is done through a bright flash of light.
    • Each charge only affects 1 attack. Weapon moves that attack multiple times have the entire attack affected.
    • These light charges last for 1 hour.
  • Gain +1 ranged weapon optimal range.
  • Gain +3 night vision skill.

Revelation of the Sun:

The humble light flare, taken to its extreme, is able to turn night into day.

  • Drop a blinding flare in a range of 3m with a effect radius of 3m, inflicting blinded(2) on all enemies within.
  • Through careful adjustment for your companion's retinas, this does not inflict friendly fire.
  • This effect cannot be saved against.

Metal Mastery:

Steel-making was the very first major breakthrough in pixie technology, and it was pioneered by a faery, Lady Numeria. Now, pixie smelters and smiths continuously seek to improve and create new blends of finer steel.

Tier 1:

  • Spend a focus point to inflict metal plague(1) on a target within 3m.
    • Expert level DC.
  • Gain +1 armour.
  • Gain +1 Smithing.

Tier 2:

  • Spend a focus point to inflict metal plague(1) on two targets within 3m.
    • Expert level DC.
  • Gain +1 armour.
  • Gain +2 Smithing.

Tier 3:

  • Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of Steel.
  • Spend a focus point to inflict metal plague(1) on three targets within 3m.
    • Expert level DC.
  • Gain +1 armour.
  • Gain +3 Smithing.

Revelation of Steel:

Trust in the surety of pixie steel.

  • Double all allies' armour within 3m of you for 3 turns.
  • In addition, all allies take half blunt force trauma.

Mist Mastery:

Pixie mist masters spend great effort learning how to make mist droplets of specialised chemicals that reflect light at just the right angles to render someone invisible.

Tier 1:

  • Spend a focus point to turn invisible(1).
  • Gain +1 ranged defence.
  • Gain +1 stealth skill.

Tier 2:

  • Spend a focus point to turn invisible(2).
  • Gain +2 ranged defence.
  • Gain +2 stealth skill.

Tier 3:

  • Spend a focus point to gain a Revelation of Emptiness.
  • Spend a focus point to turn invisible(50).
    • This lasts for 5 minutes.
  • Gain +3 ranged defence.
  • Gain +3 stealth skill.

Revelation of Emptiness:

A faery's martial prowess, paired alongside a pixie's intense concentration, allows for a silent and unseen prowler to haunt the battlefield.

  • Become unknowable(2).
