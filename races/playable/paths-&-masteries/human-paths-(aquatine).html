Human Paths (Aquatine)

Aquatine human paths are specific perks that unlock at levels 3. They are upgraded at levels 6 and 9. Once a perk path is chosen, you cannot take the others. The tiers do not stack with each other.

Paths represent a lifestyle your character embodies. You may choose not to take a path.

Tamira's Last Dancers

Tamira, goddess of rain, was perhaps the most popular deity of the continent. Some might argue she was even larger then New Illuminetres itself. But as the Aquatine Empire crumbled, Tamira's shrines and her dancers went with it. Even so, in the cursed and desolate wasteland that once was, rain still falls.

Once you choose the dancer life, you are bound to be always be dancing. When you dance in the rain, you lose stress. Failing to do so makes you gain stress.

Tier 1:

  • Gain +1 movement speed and defences.
  • Lose 1 hit chance.

Tier 2:

  • Gain +2 movement speed and defences.
  • Lose 2 hit chance.

Tier 3:

  • Gain +3 movement speed and defences.
  • Lose 3 hit chance.
  • Gain access to Dancer's Grief.

Dancer's Grief

  • Spend a regular action dancing.
  • All allies who can see you gain the tier 3 path buffs for 2 turns.
  • The next attack against you has disadvantage/You roll with advantage against grappling checks. This buff lasts 1 turn.
  • Does not stack.

Lost Legionnaire

The Aquatine Empire fielded some of the most disciplined and organised forces in all of the continent. The backbone of their military was the imperial legionnaire. The legionnaire wore heavy armour, bore large shields and spears, but what truly set them apart was their camaraderie. Legionnaires ate, slept and lived together as one. Brothers and sisters, not bound by blood. Lost, but never forgotten.

Once you join the legions, they never leave you. Going though a fight without being outflanked will lower stress, whilst not doing so will raise it.

Tier 1:

  • When equipped with 3 layers of armour and a helmet:
    • Gain +1 Armour.

Tier 2:

  • When equipped with 3 layers of armour and a helmet:
    • Gain +1 Armour.
  • Gain +2 kg to your equipment load thresholds.

Tier 3:

  • When equipped with 3 layers of armour and a helmet:
    • Gain +1 Armour.
    • Getting hit by opportunity/zoning attacks no longer removes movement speed.
  • Gain +2 kg to your equipment load thresholds.
  • Gain access to Pangolin Wall.

Pangolin Wall

  • Whenever an adjacent ally takes an incoming attack, you can spend your next turn's bonus action to redirect the attack to you.
  • The decision must be made before the attack is rolled.
  • If redirected, the attack is rolled against you as normal.
  • This inflicts and is subject to view cone restrictions as normal.
    • If you cannot turn to face the incoming attack, you can still redirect it, but you will be considered flanked for the attack roll.

Echoing Steppes

The hammer to the legionnaires' anvil, the cavalry fielded by the Aquatine Empire were second to none. Taking great pride in their pedigrees and horses, the cavalry men and women of the empire could move with their mounts as if they were one. Even as the fields rot and the stables turn to ash, the riders carry on. They must carry on.

Once you choose to be a rider, you must ride. Executing a successful charge on horseback will reduce stress whilst going through combat completely on foot will raise it.

Tier 1:

  • Horses ridden by you have +3 temperament, melee and ranged defences.
  • Mounting/Dismounting can now be done with bonus action.

Tier 2:

  • Horses ridden by you have +3 temperament, melee and ranged defences.
  • Mounting/Dismounting can now be done with bonus action.
  • Your saves against being dismounted are rolled with advantage, and you no longer fall prone if dismounted.

Tier 3:

  • Horses ridden by you have +3 temperament, melee and ranged defences.
  • Mounting/Dismounting can now be done with bonus action, and you gain back half of your movement speed after doing so.
  • Your saves against being dismounted are rolled with advantage, and you no longer fall prone if dismounted.
  • Gain access to Lance of the East.

Lance of the East

  • Once per combat, deal double damage with a mounted, melee weapon charge attack at the cost of destroying the weapon's shaft.
