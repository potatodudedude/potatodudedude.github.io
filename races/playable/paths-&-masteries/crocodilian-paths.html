Crocodilian Paths

Crocodilian paths are specific perks that unlock at levels 3. They are upgraded at levels 6 and 9. Once a perk path is chosen, you cannot take the others. The tiers do not stack with each other.

Paths represent a lifestyle your character embodies. You may choose not to take a path.

Berserker Rage

Berserkers represent the most fearsome warriors of the Steelscale Clan, most often seen wielding the Berserker Chain, a large and crude iron chain made to flatten a man in a single blow. A berserker seeks to meet a worthy match in battle, craving the addicting thrill of risking their life in mortal combat. Such is their bloodlust that many continue to fight even after sustaining injuries that would fell any other crocodilian.

Once you choose the berserker life, you are required to always go out in a blaze of glory when facing a Moment of Mortality. You lose stress from getting heavily wounded, and gain stress if you didn't lose at least half your health during combat.

Tier 1:

  • On taking damage: Gain +1 hit chance and damage on your next attack.
  • Gain +1 Strength/Dexterity when below 25% health.
  • Reaper's Grace now activates as long as you have more than 1 health.

Tier 2:

  • On taking damage: Gain +2 hit chance and damage on your next attack.
  • Gain +2 Strength/Dexterity when below 25% health.
  • Reaper's Grace now activates as long as you have more than 1 health.

Tier 3:

  • On taking damage: Gain +3 hit chance and damage on your next attack.
  • Gain +3 Strength/Dexterity when below 25% health.
  • Reaper's Grace now activates as long as you have more than 1 health.
  • Gain access to the Berserker Death State.

Berserker Death State

  • On taking fatal damage: Gain up to half your maximum health as temporary health. Gain +3 to a core stat of your choice. After taking 3 turns you die, regardless of your health.
  • During this "berserker death state", you may choose to activate the buffs granted by going out in a blaze of glory at anytime, but doing so will cause you to die immediately afterwards. In addition, this super turn counts towards one of your 3 turns before dying, meaning you cannot take 3 turns then go out in a blaze of glory.

Drunken Master

Many crocodilian scholars say that metal working and the advancements in medicine were the most influential discoveries the crocodilians received after making contact with the other races of the continent, but alcohol is probably the third. Drinking has become something of a way of life, and strange things often happen to those who drink.

Once you choose the alcoholic life, you are required to always be drinking. Going a long rest without alcohol or fighting whilst sober will make you gain stress. The big drinker trait is taken automatically. You are refunded a tier 1 trait of your choice if you already have big drinker prior to taking this mastery.

Tier 1:

  • Drinking alcohol now heals for 1d4 health.
  • During level 3 drunkenness, you no longer suffer from complications when succeeding.

Tier 2:

  • Drinking alcohol now heals for 1d6 health.
  • During level 2 and 3 drunkenness, you no longer suffer from complications when succeeding.

Tier 3:

  • Drinking alcohol now heals for 1d8 health.
  • You no longer suffer any downsides when drunk. When sober, you suffer downsides equivalent to regular level 2 drunkenness.
  • Gain access to Accidental Genius.

Accidental Genius

  • Once per combat, during level 3 drunkenness, turn any successful enemy melee attack against you back at the attacker. This will always hit.
  • Alternatively, with the same restrictions, turn any failed attack you make into a success.

Wave Harpooner

Fishing is a staple of crocodilian culture, and the most common way to do so by far is harpooning. Many also go after larger quarries in the open seas, slowing and reeling in their prey with harpoons before finishing them with their powerful jaws. Life amongst the waves teaches crocodilians a sense of ebb and flow that translates to a flexible fighting style.

Once you choose the wave harpooner life, you are required to always be wave harpooning. Spending too long away from water will make you gain stress, whilst swimming and other such aquatic activities will make you lose stress.

Tier 1:

  • Thrown weapons such as Amber Lances, Javelins and Throwing Axes gain +1 optimal range.
  • Gain +1 on swimming, fishing and stealth checks when in the water.

Tier 2:

  • Thrown weapons such as Amber Lances, Javelins and Throwing Axes gain +2 optimal range.
  • Gain +2 on swimming, fishing and stealth checks when in the water.

Tier 3:

  • Thrown weapons such as Amber Lances, Javelins and Throwing Axes gain +3 optimal range.
  • Gain advantage on swimming, fishing and stealth checks when in the water.
  • Gain access to Tide Rider.

Tide Rider

  • Gain +2 damage to your next melee attack on a successful ranged attack.
  • Gain +2 damage to your next ranged attack on a successful melee attack.
  • Buffs only last 1 turn.
