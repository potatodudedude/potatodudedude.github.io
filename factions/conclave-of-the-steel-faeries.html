Conclave of the Steel Faeries

Background:

Thanks to their symbiotic relationship with Pixies, Faeries are the most technologically advanced race on the continent. Boasting better metallurgy and craftsmanship than any other race, faeries are also known for their use of gunpowder, albeit not widespreadly. Their single city, The Citadel, is one of the most efficient, clean and comfortable to live in. Although smaller in size, it rivals even Stonebasin with a population close to a million. The steel faeries once held dominion over all lands east of the Numerian mountains, but after a disastrous defeat to a human coalition in the War of the Meadows, they were pushed back to the isle of the Ear. A tentative peace period settled, and a new status quo emerged to be undisturbed for centuries.

Culture:

The conclave is a republic with three head senators sharing power. Elections are held every 20 years. The latest senators are Yosia Numen, Tory Allador and Ellyia Olldenhammer. The new conclave was founded after the steel faeries' defeat in the war of the meadows in 627, leading to the ousting and subsequent execution of then dictator Montegra Allera. The faeries mainly worship The Old Wildwoods, although more and more are turning agnostic in the wake of the metallurgy revolution.

Steel faeries are largely isolationist, only maintaining an embassy fort on the mainland. Advanced technology, the relative abundance of the Ear and their use of sea mining allows them to be largely self-sufficient.

The first Faerian ruler and founder of the old Faerian empire, Lady Numeria, is idolised and revered almost as a saint.

Military:

The conclave practices mandatory military service for faery and pixie alike. On the battlefield, faeries utilise their superior mobility to deny and outflank the enemy, using their pixie's alchemic abilities to sow chaos and carve bloody holes in the enemy frontlines.

Elite troops can often be seen wielding numerian blades, popularised by Lady Numeria herself, dancing around the battlefield delivering quick and deadly cuts. The powder corps of the conclave wield the musket and pistol. Not many have heard the thunder of pixie gunpowder, but those that have remember it for the rest of their lives.

Ethnicity:

Steel faeries and pixies are predominantly pale in skin colour with dark hair and eyes, although it is not uncommon for higher class faeries to engage in cosmetic surgery to change their appearance.

Language:

Faeries are the ones who created the Numerian tongue, being the original occupants of the Numerian region. Due to this, the Numerian tongue has an incredibly long history, and also serves as the lingua franca of the continent.
