Steelscale Clan

Background:

Native to the tropical serpent archipelagos in the north, the Steelscale Clan are a faction of Crocodilians. The crocodilians were originally split amongst many different petty clans, until they were united under the Steelscales in 1160. Whilst usually aggressive and clamouring for war, the new generation of Steelscale leadership is more temperate, opening the crocodilians up to trade and even emigration.

Culture:

Crocodilians practice a totalitarian rule. As a compromise to prevent constant bloodshed over leadership, crocodilians practice trail by combat to determine the leading clan, known as the Blood Rites. Held every two years, each greater clan sends their strongest representative in a free-for-all to the death. A winner of the Blood Rites receives great honour and status, although the clan leaders who actually run things are usually not warriors. Despite their love for battle, the crocodilians understand the need for other strengths amongst their higher ups.

Crocodilian religious worship depends on the clan, but most clans, including the leading Steelscales, practice The Way of the Sea, preferring to live their lives in the moment, rather than devoting worship to a god.

Military:

The crocodilian military is not standardised, with most clans having their own distinctive fighting style. However, axes and throwing weapons are the most commonly seen amongst the ranks of soldiers. Crocodilians lack access to good smiths and metal, and so usually wear lighter armour.

Elite warriors of the Steelscale clan wield the terrifying berserker chain, a massive thick chain of crude iron, it's unpredictable swings are able to flatten foes in a single blow. Elite skirmishers from the Amber clan utilise the amber lance, a heavy javelin that has enough mass to punch a hole clean through the shield of an opposing soldier, the soldier himself and the poor guy behind him.

Ethnicity:

Crocodilians are most distinct from each other by the colour of their scales, with dark dull greens and browns being the most common.

Language:

Crocodilians speak the crocodilian tongue. Their dialects are the most varied amongst the major factions. Leaders and merchants may learn the Numerian tongue, but most common folk don't.
