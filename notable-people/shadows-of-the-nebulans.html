Shadows of the Nebulans

The Shadows of the Nebulans are a group of various people from the continent of the laughing titan who chose to side with the Nebulans, conducting sabotage and helping the Nebulans scout out the continent before the invasion. They were condemned as traitors after the Tyranny of the Stars.

Curiously, despite their involvement in events leading up to the invasion, the Shadows of the Nebulans were not present for it. After the Nebulans were routed off, The Last Legion discovered their remains on the Isle of Andromeda, located between the continent of The Laughing Titan and the continent of the Eye of the Storm. They were found to have died fighting alongside The Canaries, against the Nebulans. It's not known why they were attacked, but many speculate they were either betrayed by the Nebulans and decided to go out fighting, or The Canaries persuaded them to abandon their goal and fight for the Laughing Titan instead.

Gehang Mannlicher

Gehang was elected as a high overseer after The East-West War, and served to try and repair the damage done to the reputation of New Illuminetres during the war. About 6 months before the Tyranny of the Stars, Gehang embarked on a pilgrimage around the world. Inviting representatives from each kingdom aboard his ship, he aimed to sail around the continent as a mark of friendship and cooperation between the kingdoms. Ulysses and Basilan were aboard this voyage as representatives of the Conclave of the Steel Faeries. However, when their ship left The Left Claw for The Right Claw, they turned up a month late, missing majority of the people on board, except for Gehang, Ulysses and Basilan, and a handful of Numerian sailors. The story told then was that there had been a freak storm that had washed away most of the crew, and the situation was a huge scandal. With hindsight, many believe the ship was abducted by the Nebulans, and this was when Gehang, Ulysses and Basilan struck a deal to work with the Nebulans in their conquest of the continent.

Gehang is suspected to be the one responsible for rallying the remaining disillusioned fanatics of New Illuminetres under his banner, sending Thaddeus and the false fishermen to incite chaos in The Right Claw to stop the continent from discovering the Nebulans before the invasion occured. He is also believed to be the one originally responsible for the plot to capture ships off the northern coast and use them as Trojan horses to sneak the Nebulans past the defences of The Citadel.

Gehang's exact motivation for siding with the Nebulans remains unknown, but many theorise he did it to cut a deal, having the Nebulans agree to spare the Numerians in exchange for conquering the rest of the continent. It's believed he is the reason the Numerians were spared the brunt of the invasion.

His body was found alongside his personal paladin guards on the Isle of Andromeda.

Thaddeus and the false fishermen

Thaddeus was a former paladin during The East-West War, rumoured to have served alongside Alexander Godfrey. Decades later, he was the one in charge of leading a band of numerian fanatics under order from Gehang Mannlicher to sow chaos in The Right Claw and sabotage investigations that could jeopardise the Nebulan war plans. Having received word of a group of numerian fishermen headed for the Right Claw, Thaddeus used the authority of Gehang to delay them, allowing his fanatics to go in posing as the fishermen. Responsible for the Insurrection at the Claw, Thaddeus would eventually meet his end in a confrontation with Canary squad and his old battle brother, Alexander Godfrey, during the the burning of the Claw. He was slain by the spear of Fregsworth of the Canary squad, who gave his life in the process.

Utari Sanjaya

A native Aquatine, Utari was a town guard in The Right Claw, playing a pivotal role in the fanatic's plans during the Insurrection at the Claw. She was the one responsible for the shooting of a fanatic turncoat, alongside passing information on where to source gunpowder ingredients to the fanatics. Furthermore, she was the one who hired Doberman squad and lured them into an ambush as a wider ploy to cripple the Blue Hounds and prevent their investigation into the Nebulans' activities.

Due to Canary squad's investigations, she was eventually outed and arrested. Shortly after The Canaries left to sail north, she was executed for treason.

Her motivations for aiding New Illuminetres fanatics despite being born an Aquatine citizen remains somewhat muddled. When questioned, her parents said they had disowned her after her failures in the trade exams. It's believed she was found and indoctrinated by Numerian fanatics, who then used her to infiltrate the guards at the Right Claw.

Ulysses and Basilan

Former members of the powder corps in the Conclave of the Steel Faeries, steel faery Ulysses and his pixie Basilan were exceptional soldiers, masters of alchemy and martial arts. Strikingly, Ulysses always wears a silver mask covering the left half of his face which is horrifically disfigured, whilst Basilan has his two right wings replaced with silver prosthetics. Furthermore, Ulysses' eyes have no irises. The duo were originally exiles who earned their way back into the conclave due to their incredible prowess. Although they served for distinction for many years, their true loyalties were revealed during the months leading up to the Tyranny of the Stars.

The two were part of the cursed voyage by Gehang Mannlicher, and were also part of the security detail of the faery trade ship, the Grey Manta, which was attacked at sea and ran aground. It's believed they assisted the Nebulans in attacking the Grey Manta, and are also the ones who provided the New Illuminetres fanatics with the knowledge to make lesser gunpowder for their attacks during the Insurrection at the Claw.

When the Tyranny of the Stars began, a small number of Nebulan ships were outfitted with gunpowder cannons, believed to have been made for them by the duo.

Their motivations are ultimately unknown, but it's believed that they were exiled from the Conclave of the Steel Faeries due to their physical disabilities, leading them to plot their revenge and collude with the Nebulans to destroy The Citadel. They are believed to be responsible for the Nebulans targeting The Citadel at the very start of the war.

Like the others, Ulysses and Basilan were found dead on the Isle of Andromeda, apparently having died fighting the Nebulans.

Assorted Mercernaries

Besides the traitor faery and pixie and fanatics, it's also believed Gehang used his wealth to hire various mercenary groups and ships to aid in his plots. Whether these mercenaries knew they were aiding the conquest of the continent remains a matter of debate.
