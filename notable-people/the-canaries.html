The Canaries

This group of scouts was awarded the name of "The Canaries" posthumously, after they gave their lives to bring valuable intel and forewarning of the Nebulan invasion. Their efforts contributed to The Right Claw's proper defence and holdout against the Nebulans, as well as allowing the Conclave of the Steel Faeries to synthesis a counter to Stardust earlier, preventing further destruction and death. Much of their story remains unknown as they perished on the Isle of Andromeda, far north of the continent. Strangely, they were found to have died fighting alongside the Shadows of the Nebulans against the invaders. Many speculate the two opposing parties found common ground in the end. Their remains were only discovered by The Last Legion after the Nebulans had already been repelled.

Most of the accounts about the Canaries come from Captain Hong Li Ma, captain of the town guards in the Right Claw, as well as various townsfolk who survived the invasion. After the war, Captain Li Ma set up a memorial to them in the town, marked by a distinctive blue oak sapling, a small beacon of hope in the wasteland of Marander Gas.

Canary Squad

Canary squad is a reconnaissance squad under the larger mercenary company, the Blue Hounds. A team of 5 various greenhorns and their handler Charlie, Canary squad was formed less than a month before the Tyranny of the Stars in order to help investigate missing ships off the coast of The Right Claw.

Playing a major role in uncovering the Numerian fisherman conspiracy during the Insurrection at the Claw, they helped uncover traitor guardswomen Utari Sanyaja as an agent of the Shadows of the Nebulans. In addition, they discovered an encampment outside the town being used as storage for materials to create lesser gunpowder for the New Illuminetres fanatics and slew their leader, Thaddeus, in combat during the burning of the Claw.

After the dust settled, Canary squad sailed north with the rest of the Canaries, investigating clues left behind by the fanatics. Days before the Nebulan Invasion, two ravens were sent by the Canaries. One was sent to The Right Claw, warning of imminent invasion by a massive fleet of foreign creatures, capable of secreting a deadly mist. Because of this, The Right Claw remains one of the few places properly prepared for the invasion. Without the Canaries' warning, the coastal town would likely have been destroyed in the first night of the invasion. The second was sent to The Citadel, with detailed plans of the Nebulans' attack plans, crucially warning of a Nebulan ploy to use the missing ships as a Trojan horse to weaken the Citadel's defences. Alongside this message, Canary squad had also managed to send a small container containing a sample of Stardust. This container, named "The Cage of the Stars" remains the only known vessel able to contain Stardust. Thanks to their intel, the Citadel was able to foil the Nebulan's Trojan horse plans, and begin research on Stardust early, leading to the reaction turning Stardust into Marander Gas being discovered much earlier. Strangely, a blue acorn was also sent with the raven, but the significance of this is not known. The acorn was later planted at their memorial site in The Right Claw.

Despite their contributions, Canary squad never returned to the continent. It's believed they gave their lives to gather the information they sent. The squad consisted of:

Fregsworth

A steel faery, Fregsworth is described as a timid person, often trying to hide his faery wings under a cloak. He exiled himself from the Citadel, joining Canary squad in hopes of finding his pixie, Llyod, who had also left. Despite his shy nature and propensity to default to authority, Fregsworth would show bravery in the moments where it mattered most. During The Burning of the Claw, Fregsworth gave his life to slay Thaddeus, leader of the New Illuminetres fanatics.

Llyod

A steel pixie, Llyod was originally bonded to Fregsworth. Most describe him as being rather obnoxious and difficult to work with, as well as having a strange accent. The Conclave of the Steel Faeries report him as a problematic individual who sought to break the nature of faery-pixie relationships, seeking independence for pixies alone. Despite this, he played an important role in the investigation into gunpowder production by the New Illuminetres fanatics, and saved the other members of Canary squad on occasion using his mastery over light. After Fregsworth's death, he was bonded to Zodiac.

Wonnie

A Wingardian from the sands of the south, Wonnie joined Canary squad as a way of furthering his medical research. Having been taught by a unorthodox doctor, he showed a somewhat concerning interest in the brewing of different drugs and medicines, as well as the different anatomy of the various other races. Reported to have been absorbed in investigating a strange substance left by his mentor, Wonnie was an eccentric but valuable member of the squad.

Jacob “Jacobi” Jacobsson

A young Numerian boy who had apparently lied about his age to get onto the squad, Jacob was an optimistic boy, an avid bookworm and a believer in New Illuminetres. Curious to a fault, Jacob left home and joined Canary squad to explore the world. He would face the ugly side of his faith directly during the Insurrection at the Claw. Local firekeeper in the Right Claw, Renly Smith, laments that he was too young to be partaking in his dangerous mission, but admits he was brave for one facing such horrors at such an age.

Rangarangarang

A crocodilian of the Steelscale Clan, Rangarangarang joined Canary squad in the hopes of testing and proving himself, independent of his family. A boisterous fellow, he is described as direct and strange, known to argue at length over 2 gold pieces. Also well known is his large appetite for alcohol, often seen hauling around egregious amounts of beer, and rumoured to have participated in a drinking competition with goblins. Though rough around the edges, his heart was in the right place.

Kara

A young Aquatine citizen, Kara set out to join Canary squad to widen her world view and seek adventure. Her parents describe her to be chasing after her mentor, who vanished trying to investigate the Red Oaks for a way to further the agriculture of the empire. A person chasing the shadows of her past, Kara started as an inexperienced wide-eyed rookie, but proved herself in her actions defending and protecting the continent. It's rumoured the blue acorn was sent by her.

Gustav “Zodiac” Herschel

A scout of the steel faeries' powder corps, Zodiac lost his pixie in combat and was brought aboard Canary squad after Fregsworth death to bond with Llyod. Having limited interactions with the people of the Right Claw, his powder corps records detail him as a straight-laced soldier, obedient and unquestioning to a fault. He served well, laying down his life on the Isle of Andromeda alongside the rest of Canary Squad.

Charlie

The handler for Canary squad, Charlie is the only member who had been working in the Blue Hounds before the formation of Canary squad. Described as an industrious fellow, he is most often seen in the town post office collating intel and writing letters begging the Blue Hounds higher ups for more resources. At times, townsfolk also see him being dragged out of town by the rest of his squad during investigations, despite being a non-combatant himself.

Doberman Squad

Doberman squad is a combat squad of 4 crocodilians under the larger Blue Hounds mercenary company. Operating independently of a handler, Doberman squad was hired by the traitor guardswoman Utari Sanjaya to destroy a bandit camp near the Right Claw that doesn't exist. As part of the greater ploy by the New Illuminetres fanatics to weaken Blue Hounds and stop their investigation into the missing vessels, Doberman squad was ambushed enroute, suffering one killed and one grievously wounded. Despite their losses, Doberman squad killed their ambushers, and proceeded to aid in the takedown of the fanatics during the burning of the Claw, and joining Canary squad when they sailed north. Like Canary squad, Doberman squad was killed on the Isle of Andromeda getting information to the continent. Their members are:

Acrila Maneater

Described to be freakishly large, even for a female crocodilian, Acrila is the leader of Doberman squad. A berserker of the Steelscale Clan, Acrila wields their terrifying Berserker Chain, embodying the larger than life rowdiness and ferocity of a berserker. Many accounts detail her brushing off serious injuries that would put down lesser beings, and revelling in the heat of battle.

Killian Ironjaw

The second in command of Doberman squad and veteran of numerous missions, Killian is described as a stoic person, having lost an eye during his various battles in service of the Redclaw clan. Representing the discipline to Acrila's fury, he was often seen going over maps and plans with the local guards.

Yoholk Breaker

A youngblood from the Steelscale Clan, Yoholk was a promising warrior getting real battle experience working for Doberman squad. He gave his life saving Acrila from a hail of arbalest bolts when the squad was ambushed by the fanatics.

Rinnie Tiderider

Another youngblood, this time from the Amberclan, Rinnie was an elite lance thrower. She suffered grevious injuries during the ambush by the fanatics and was left behind to recover when the Canaries sailed north. She is the only surviving member of Doberman squad.

Pollie Usted and Felixanderilyacobrutusimoniago the twelfth

Elite scouts from the powder corps of the Conclave of the Steel Faeries, steel faery Pollie Usted and her pixie Felixanderilyacobrutusimoniago the twelfth (real name of Iago) were sent to the Right Claw to investigate the missing faery trade ship, the Grey Manta. An elusive duo, their exact involvement in the events at the Claw remains unknown. Their records within the Citadel details them as hailing from the prestigious House Ourobous, the latest in a long line of famous warriors. Skilled with musket, gunpowder and stealth alike, the duo accompanied Canary squad when they sailed north, and likewise did not return.

Alexander Godfrey

A former Numerian paladin in The East-West War, Alexander has since turned to mercenary work. Despite his past allegiances to the fanatics of New Illuminetres, he helped quell the uprising during the burning of the Claw, and accompanied Canary squad on their voyage north. Local firekeeper Renly Smith describes him as a grizzled old man with a gaze that seemed to stretch until the end of the world. He is rumoured to be former comrades in arms with Thaddeus, leader of the fanatics, the two meeting again on opposite sides of the conflict.

Kelissa Marines

Headed by Captain Yacob Said, these Wingardian marines were sent to the Right Claw aboard the warship, Kelissa, in order to investigate missing Wingardian trade vessels. Highly disciplined and wielding their distinctive falces, they were the first target of the fanatics when the Kelissa was bombed and rendered unseaworthy. Despite this, they proved instrumental in helping repel the fanatics during the the burning of the Claw, and accompanied Canary squad aboard a borrowed Aquatine patrol boat, the Lance of Urumi, as an armed escort when they sailed north.
