Introduction

Hello! Welcome to my homemade wiki!

Bear in mind this wiki will likely undergo further changes as I try to balance the game and fill out the lore, sorry for the inconvenience!

How to play this TTRPG? Gameplay usually boils down into a simple loop:

  1. The GM describes a situation you are in.
  2. You decide what to do with the rest of the party.
  3. Depending on what you do and how well you do it, the GM describes a new situation.
  4. Repeat from step 1.

There are different rules that determine how well/terribly you do something. See Rolling Dice, Character Sheet & Stats to see how these are determined.

During combat, there are a number of rules that you need to be aware of. See Melee Attacks, Ranged Attacks, Actions, Rolling Dice, Initiative and Turn Order and Damage Calculations.

Don't be too worried about memorising every single rule you see, I'll do my best to ease new players into the system one step at a time. Don't be afraid to ask questions or clarify if needed?

For character creation and stats, visit Character Creation, Character Sheet & Stats and Levels.

For general lore, visit the race, faction, notable concepts/events/people folders.

Changes

1.09

  • Lower movement speed across all races, accounting for relatively small map sizes.
  • Changes to skills system. Currently skills are made for vague roleplaying, not bound by strict rules. This will be changed to have a core of practical, rule bound skills, followed by a bunch of vague roleplaying skills.
    • Skill point allocation will change accordingly to ensure balanced character skill compositions.
  • Massive overhaul to crafting system. Current system is long-crafting, i.e. making things already available through shops available by crafting. The aim of the new systems is to allow players to craft their own unique and tailored gear that cannot be bought.
    • Added weapon and armour smithing, alongside a weapon parts system.
    • Added alchemy crafting for making customised medicine and potions.
    • Added ammunition crafting for making customised arrows, bolts and stones.
    • Added gunpowder chemistry for making customised grenades, firearm shots and gunpowder.
    • Existing items changed to have stats that are reproducible through crafting systems.
  • Changed devastating flanker to allow finishing other people's combo starters when flanking, and guaranteeing flanking attacks will hit.
  • Changed greatsword's multi-hit combo finisher to be more flexible.
  • Added lore entries for the canaries.
  • Added descriptions to all weapon types.
  • Changed foot charging mechanics to be consistent with horse charging mechanics.
  • Changed all tiering systems to be from 1-3 for consistency.
  • Added veterancy mechanic to allow certain stats to cap and scale with level.
  • Added horses, mounted combat rules and horse equipment.
  • Buffed neurotoxin to guarantee death after 5 turns.
  • Added time mechanics to resting and crafting.
  • Reworked initiative rules drastically to try and promote more teamwork and strategizing.
  • Added rulings for different terrains, including swimming rules.
  • Lowered costs of certain throwables and coatings.
  • Lowered costs of gunpowder items and increased firearm shot crafting efficiency.
  • Changed addiction to use exhaustion mechanics.
  • Added lingering area of effect rules.
  • Attempting to grapple will now make you subject to an attack of opportunity if the conditions are right.
  • Added race paths, sort of subclasses for races at level 3.
  • Adjusted shields to have the option to use stat scaling for defences instead of only a hard value.
  • Adjusted dual-wielder trait to give +1 hit chance when dual wielding.
  • Added minor and major complications and added more clear rulings for certain complications.
    • Most situations will use major complications.
    • Once tier 3 proficiency is reached, minor complications will be used for attacks with complications/nat 1s instead.
  • Changed weapon wielding rules to remove potentially unclear terms.
  • Changed arbalest slowing shot to have expert DC to account for slow fire rate.
  • Changed bodkin ammunition to have damage penalties with a higher penetration bonus whilst broadhead ammunition now has penetration penalties with a damage bonus.
  • Added stat scaling to damage of all weapons.
  • Adjusted bandages bleeding amount to account for the lowered mass bleed threshold.
  • Added label of grappling moves to distinguish between grappling moves and attack moves that can be used when grappled.
  • Changed charging move bonuses to activate every 3m instead due to relatively small size of maps, and changed charge vulnerability against opp/zoning attacks to use the charge bonuses for simplicity.
  • Changed weapon moves that move enemies/user to use normal attack hit chance, damage, penetration and BFT values to encourage more movement tactics.
  • Buffed mace debuff moves to last longer.

1.06

  • Armour values are too high for most weapons' penetration, so I'm nerfing it.
    • Layer 1 armours now don't give any armour value, but are needed to wear layer 2 and 3 armours.
    • Faeries get a free arming jacket in their initial equipment to account for them not having layer 1 armours before.
  • Crocodilian unarmed bonuses are getting reworked.
    • To make punching and biting more distinct, punches no longer deal additional damage, but can inflict dazed.
    • Bites have their bonus damage nerfed, capping to +2.
  • Beer gives too much benefit for no downside during combat, so I am adding side effects.
    • Being drunk now penalises hit chance.
  • Dexterity bonuses to defences are too stackable given the rounding-up system, so the dual-wielding defence calculations are being changed.
    • Now, only the melee defence from one of your weapon applies when using two weapons.
  • In addition, melee and ranged defence bonuses from being in light load are now capped at +2.
  • Fixed zoning typo on rapiers.
  • Hand crossbows are now able to fire multiple times and are no longer be subject to melee debuffs.
  • Agile trait is buffed to add dexterity to ranged defence when wielding specific weapons.
  • Changed short sword pommel smash to use dexterity as BFT.
  • Changed thick skin to reduce BFT by your endurance modifier.
  • Added false dragon trait.
  • Buffed fast adapter to give +2 hit chance on miss.

1.04

  • Helmets have been reworked. They used to give too much armour for the weight, so I tried to rebalance them in a way that still makes sense.
    • For clarity, tiers 1, 2 and 3 are renamed to light, medium and heavy, since the tiering name implies a tier 3 helm is better, when it is no longer necessarily the case.
    • Light helms provide 1 armour only if you have light equipment load.
    • Medium helms provide 1 armour with no caveats.
    • Heavy helms provide 3 armour but restrict your view cone.
    • Aquatines now start with a light helm instead.
  • Added minimum BFT of 1 to weapon moves that use player stats to deal BFT.
  • Reduced the price of toolkits to make them more accessible.
  • Nerfed flails so missing will only deal half the BFT.
  • Adjusted some inconsistencies with the 2-handed flail.
  • I forgot to account for OP combo spam with altan brew, it now only lasts 1 turn but the side effects are less severe.
  • Added concrete DCs for addiction.
  • Added relevant lore for the Right Claw during the East-West War.
  • Added lore entries for the War of the Meadows, Augustine the Third and Lady Numeria.
