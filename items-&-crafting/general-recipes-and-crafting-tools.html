General Recipes and Crafting Tools

Each crafting recipe takes 1 time-unit to complete.
Crafting is usually cheaper than buying the same item, but bear in mind items only sell back to traders for 50% normally, so don't get carried away trying to play tycoon simulator.

The following lists contains recipes that are considered "long buying", meaning they are just ways for you to acquire items you could already buy but just cheaper/with different resources. See the skilled crafting folder for more in depth systems that allow you to customise your creations.

Smithing Tools

  • Allows repairing/refitting metal armour and weapons.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 200g

You can repair/adjust armour that specifies the smithing skill as their repair/adjustment requirement.

If not otherwise stated, the skill requirement is as such:

  • Tier 1 Adjustment Score: No skill needed
  • Tier 2 Adjustment Score: Level 1 smithing skill
  • Tier 3 Adjustment Score: Level 2 smithing skill
  • Tier 4+ Adjustment Score: Level 3 smithing skill

The time-unit cost and material cost is equal to twice the armour's adjustment score. You do not have to perform the entire repair/adjustment in one sitting, you can split it across multiple sittings.

Recipes

5 Metal Bolts
  • Requires 5 metal bars.
  • Requires level 2 smithing(weapons).

Tailoring Tools

  • Allows repairing/refitting leather/cloth armour and clothes.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 200g

You can repair/adjust armour that specifies the tailoring skill as their repair/adjustment requirement.

If not otherwise stated, the skill requirement is as such:

  • Tier 1 Adjustment Score: Level 0 tailoring skill
  • Tier 2 Adjustment Score: Level 1 tailoring skill
  • Tier 3 Adjustment Score: Level 2 tailoring skill
  • Tier 4+ Adjustment Score: Level 3 tailoring skill

The time-unit cost and material cost is equal to twice the armour's adjustment score. You do not have to perform the entire repair/adjustment in one sitting, you can split it across multiple sittings.

Recipes

1 Marander Mask (Makeshift)
  • Requires 1 marander filter, 1 cloth/linen/wool and 1 leather.
  • Requires level 1 tailoring
