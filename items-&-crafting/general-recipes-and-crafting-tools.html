General Recipes and Crafting Tools

Each crafting recipe takes 1 time-unit to complete.
Crafting is usually cheaper than buying the same item, but bear in mind items only sell back to traders for 50% normally, so don't get carried away trying to play tycoon simulator.

The following lists contains recipes that are considered "long buying", meaning they are just ways for you to acquire items you could already buy but just cheaper/with different resources. See the skilled crafting folder for more in depth systems that allow you to customise your creations.

Cooking Implements

  • Allows cooking more complicated foods. Recipes require fresh ingredients.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 200g

Recipes

1 Bread
  • Requires 1 raw grain.
  • Requires level 2 cooking skill.
1 Cooked Rice
  • Requires 1 raw rice.
  • Requires level 1 cooking skill.
1 Hard Tack
  • Requires 1 raw grain.
  • Alternatively, require 1 raw rice.
  • Requires level 1 cooking skill.
1 Raw Fish
  • Requires 1 salted fish.
  • Requires level 1 cooking skill.
1 Raw Meat
  • Requires 1 salted meat.
  • Requires level 1 cooking skill.
1 Salted Fish
  • Requires 1 raw fish and 1 salt.
  • Requires level 2 cooking skill.
  • Salt is not used up.
1 Salted Meat
  • Requires 1 raw meat and 1 salt.
  • Requires level 2 cooking skill.
  • Salt is not used up.
1 Smoked Corpse Fungus
  • Requires 1 corpse fungus and 1 timber.
  • Requires level 2 cooking skill.
1 Smoked Fish
  • Requires 1 raw fish and 1 timber.
  • Requires level 2 cooking skill.
1 Smoked Meat
  • Requires 1 raw meat and 1 timber.
  • Requires level 2 cooking skill.
3 Faery Bread
  • Requires 1 raw grain, 1 raw meat and 1 faery reagent.
  • Alternatively, replace raw meat with any other type of meat or fish.
  • Requires level 3 cooking skill.
1 Fish Sauce
  • Requires 1 raw fish and 1 salt.
  • Alternatively, require 1 salted fish.
  • Requires level 3 cooking skill.

Moonshining Equipment

  • Allows brewing your own alcohol.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 200g

Recipes

1 Beer
  • Requires 1 raw grain and 1 bottle.
  • Requires level 1 brewing.
1 Wine
  • Requires 4 fruit and 1 bottle.
  • Alternatively, require 8 cooked rice and 1 bottle.
  • Requires level 2 brewing.
1 Moonshine
  • Requires 2 raw grain and 1 bottle.
  • Alternatively, replace raw grain with raw rice
  • Requires level 2 brewing.
1 Concentrated Alcohol
  • Requires 2 moonshine.
  • Alternatively, require 3 wine or 4 beer.
  • Requires level 2 brewing.

Alchemy Equipment

  • Allows brewing your own potions.
  • See Alchemy and Gunpowder Chemistry.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 200g

Pixie Alchemy Equipment

  • Allows brewing your own potions, unlocks certain reactions.
  • See Alchemy and Gunpowder Chemistry.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 1000g

Smithing Tools

  • Allows crafting and repairing/refitting armour and weapons.
  • See Armour Smithing, Weapon Smithing and Ammunition Crafting.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 200g

Recipes

1 Steel Bar
  • Requires 2 iron bars.
  • Requires level 2 weapon/armour smithing.

Pixie Smithing Tools

  • Allows crafting and repairing/refitting armour and weapons, unlocks certain customisations.
  • See Armour Smithing, Weapon Smithing and Ammunition Crafting.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 1000g

Recipes

Any recipes that can be made with the regular smithing tools can be made with this version.

1 Pixie-Forged Steel Bar
  • Requires 2 steel bars.
  • Requires level 3 weapon/armour smithing or a pixie with metal mastery.

Tailoring Tools

  • Allows crafting and repairing of clothing.
  • See Tailoring.
  • Weight: 10kg
  • Cost: 100g
