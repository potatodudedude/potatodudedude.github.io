Food

Preservation and Spoilage

Food has 3 tiers of spoilage:

  • Fresh: Yummers.
  • Spoiling: Make a DC 12 endurance save or the effects of the food are negated, and you gain minor food poisoning.
  • Rotten: Make a DC 20 endurance save or the effects of the food are negated, and you gain major food poisoning.

Each food item has a spoilage timer. After a fresh food item's spoilage timer expires, it will progress to become spoiling. After another day, it will become rotten.

Variety

Each food item has a variety value. A meal made during long rest can give benefits depending on variety.

Each food type can only confer its variety bonus once.

Communal Cooking

Usually, you can only eat 2 units of food, limiting the variety of your meal. However, you may choose to make a communal meal.
Communal meals can achieve greater variety by allowing you to cook everyone's portions together, thus using more ingredients.
This requires a cooking check of DC 5 + variety of the meal. Failing this check imposes a 1d4 penalty to the variety of the final meal.

Variety Bonuses:

These bonuses are subject to change depending on the size of the group.

5 members:
  • Bland (<2): No bonuses.
  • Decent (2-4): -1 Stress.
  • Sumptuous (5-8): -2 Stress, +1d4 temporary health.
  • Decadent (8-10): -3 Stress, +1d6 temporary health and +1 to any core stat of your choice until next long rest.
  • Fit for a king (>10): -4 Stress, +1d8 temporary health and +2 to any core stat of your choice until next long rest.
3-4 members:
  • Bland (<2): No bonuses.
  • Decent (2-3): -1 Stress.
  • Sumptuous (4-5): -2 Stress, +1d4 temporary health.
  • Decadent (6-8): -3 Stress, +1d6 temporary health and +1 to any core stat of your choice until next long rest.
  • Fit for a king (>8): -4 Stress, +1d8 temporary health and +2 to any core stat of your choice until next long rest.
<3 members:
  • Bland (<1): No bonuses.
  • Decent (1-2): -1 Stress.
  • Sumptuous (3-4): -2 Stress, +1d4 temporary health.
  • Decadent (4-6): -3 Stress, +1d6 temporary health and +1 to any core stat of your choice until next long rest.
  • Fit for a king (>6): -4 Stress, +1d8 temporary health and +2 to any core stat of your choice until next long rest.

Alcohol

You cannot survive off of alcohol alone. Each unit of alcohol weighs 1kg. Drinking takes a bonus action/regular action.

Beer

  • Grants 1 level of drunkenness if drunk.
  • Cost per unit: 1g

Wine

  • Grants 1 levels of drunkenness if drunk.
  • Variety: 1
  • Acts as an accoutrement.
  • Cost per unit: 100g

Moonshine

  • Grants 2 levels of drunkenness if drunk.
  • Not normally sold in towns.
  • Cost per unit: 10g

Concentrated Alcohol

  • Grants 3 levels of drunkenness if drunk.
  • Not normally sold in towns.
  • Cost per unit: 50g
