Two-handed Maces

Simple in concept, a metal ball on the end of a stick. Despite this, maces can be crude or intricately ornate. Capable of delivering serious blunt force, maces are the premier choice for cracking heavy armour.

Hand requirement: Two handed
Unique traits: -

Base Stats:

Hit chance: Strength + Proficiency
Damage: 1d8
Penetration: 2 x Strength (Capped by 2 x Proficiency)
Blunt Force Trauma: Strength (Min 1)
Total Range: 1m
Optimal Range: 1m
Melee Defence: 0
Weight: 4kg
Cost: 520g

Crafting:

Parts:

  • Heavy
    • Material: 8 metal units
    • Craft Time: 6 time-units
    • Cost: 480g
    • Weight: 2kg
  • Short-Haft
    • Material: 2 wood unit
    • Craft Time: 4 time-units
    • Cost: 40g
    • Weight: 2kg

Moveset:

Values are organised as such: (Hit chance | Damage | Penetration | BFT | Range)
Values below serve as add-ons to the weapon's base stats, apart from range.
"S" or "D" refers to your strength or dexterity modifier.
"S|D" means to use either strength or dexterity modifier, whichever is higher.

  • "S|D" is always used as an enclosed term, meaning terms like "S|D+1" refers to "(S|D)+1" and not "S|(D+1)".
    +0 means no bonuses or maluses. =x means the value is set to x.
    "|" is used to denote "or", "/" is used to denote divide.

Tier 1:

Swing

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • This is your default opportunity attack.

Charge!

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | finishes at 1m)

  • Regular action + movement speed.
  • See charging rules for more details.

Tier 2:

Ankle-breaker

(+S | x1/2 | x1/2 | x1/2 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Inflict broken ankles(2) on your opponent.

Wrist-breaker

(+S | x1/2 | x1/2 | x1/2 | 1m)

  • Regular Action.
  • Inflict broken wrist(2) on your opponent.

Far Swing

(+0 | x1/2 | x1/2 | x1/2 | 2m)

  • Regular action.
  • Hold your mace at the very bottom of the shaft and swing out, extending your range to 2m.

Tier 3:

Armour Breaker

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 0m)

  • Passive bonus, only applies to this weapon.
  • Add your strength modifier to the penetration stat.

Tap...

(+S | x1/2 | x1/2 | x1/2 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Inflicts pinned(1).
  • Combo starter, swing your mace and maintain momentum, preparing to ...Double Down.
  • Combo is broken if your target moves or you are moved/disabled.

...Double Down

(+S | x2 | x2 | x2 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Combo finisher, after Tap..., release a devastating hit, inflicting broken wrist(1) and broken ankles(1).
  • Expert level DC.
