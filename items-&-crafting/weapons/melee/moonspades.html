Moonspades

A polearm with a sharpened shovel head on one end and a inverted crescent blade on the other, the moonspade has humble origins as a traveler's weapon. The crescent blade allowed for handling and grappling wild animals, whilst the shovel allows for proper burial of any bandits unfortunate enough to cross the path of a wielder. The flexibility and unconventional style of fighting made it a prime choice amongst the elite shock troops of the Aquatine Empire.

Hand requirement: Two handed
Unique traits: Each move is separated into using the spade blade or the crescent blade, with synergies between the two types.
This is a faction-unique weapon, it requires specialist training from the Aquatine Empire to gain proficiency.

Moveset:

Values are organised as such: (Hit chance | Damage | Penetration | BFT | Range)
Values below serve as add-ons to the weapon's base stats, apart from range.
"S" or "D" refers to your strength or dexterity modifier.
"S|D" means to use either strength or dexterity modifier, whichever is higher.

  • "S|D" is always used as an enclosed term, meaning terms like "S|D+1" refers to "(S|D)+1" and not "S|(D+1)".
    +0 means no bonuses or maluses. =x means the value is set to x.
    "|" is used to denote "or", "/" is used to denote divide.

Tier 1:

Hew

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Spade move.
  • This is your default opportunity attack.

Zone

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Crescent move.
  • Allows you to use zoning attacks until your next turn.
  • Does not count as an opportunity attack.

Charge!

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | finishes at 1m)

  • Regular action + movement speed.
  • Spade move.
  • See charging rules for more details.

Tier 2:

Splinter

(Adv | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Spade move.
  • Inflict splintered shield(1) on your opponent if they have a shield.
  • Bucklers are not affected.
  • Expert DC.

Crescent Chariot

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Crescent move.
  • Place your crescent head on the enemy and move them back 1m whilst moving 1m forwards with them.
  • You and the target do not trigger opportunity attacks, but can trigger zoning attacks.

Far Swing

(+0 | x1/2 | x1/2 | x1/2 | 2m)

  • Regular action.
  • Spade move.
  • Hold your moonspade at the very bottom of the shaft and swing out, extending your range to 2m.

Tier 3:

Yin and Yang

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 0m)

  • Passive bonus.
  • After using a spade move, all crescent moves gain +2 hit chance and DC.
  • After using a crescent move, all spade moves gain +2 hit chance and damage.
  • It does not matter if the attacks hit or miss.
  • Bonuses are lost after 1 turn if not used.

Gaze of...

(+S|D | x1/2 | x1/2 | x1/2 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Inflicts pinned(1).
  • Counts as both spade and crescent moves.
  • Combo starter, smack your target with the middle of the shaft, setting them up for ...The Earth or ...The Moon.
  • Combo is broken if your target moves or you are moved/disabled.

...The Earth

(+S|D | x2 | x2 | x2 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Spade move.
  • Combo finisher, after Gaze of..., swing your spade to send your target to the grave.

...The Moon

(+S|D | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Crescent move.
  • Combo finisher, after Gaze of..., pin your target with your crescent and knock them prone, leaving them to stare at the sky.
  • Expert level DC.
