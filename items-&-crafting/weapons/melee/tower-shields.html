Tower Shields

A staple of the Aquatine legions, tower shields are massive shields, offering complete coverage of the user's body, at the cost of high weight and low mobility. Often used in formation, the tower shield allows the presentation of an unbreakable wall to the enemy.

Hand requirement: Off handed

Moveset:

Values are organised as such: (Hit chance | Damage | Penetration | BFT | Range)
Values below serve as add-ons to the weapon's base stats, apart from range.
"S" or "D" refers to your strength or dexterity modifier.
"S|D" means to use either strength or dexterity modifier, whichever is higher.

  • "S|D" is always used as an enclosed term, meaning terms like "S|D+1" refers to "(S|D)+1" and not "S|(D+1)".
    +0 means no bonuses or maluses. =x means the value is set to x.
    "|" is used to denote "or", "/" is used to denote divide.

Tier 1:

Shield Bash

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular/Bonus action.
  • This is your default opportunity attack.

Tier 2:

Deflecting Advance

( =0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 0m)

  • Regular action.
  • Move 1m, enemy opportunity and zoning attacks have disadvantage against you.

Towering Shove

( +S,D | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular/Bonus action.
  • Execute a powerful shove on the enemy and move them back 1m.
  • Enemies moved this way do not trigger opportunity attacks, but can trigger zoning attacks.

Castle Protocol

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 0m)

  • Regular action, toggleable.
  • Plant your shield in a defensive manner, removing your ability to move.
  • Double the defences from the shield.
  • Gain advantage against being moved, grappled and knocked prone.
