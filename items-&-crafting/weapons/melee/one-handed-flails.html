One-handed Flails

The two-handed flail was once a farm tool, used to thresh wheat in the Kingdom of Numeria. Unlike its two-handed counter part, the one-handed flail was made specifically for war. A metal ball connected to a stick by a chain, the flail swings unpredictably, bypassing an opponent's guard and dealing heavy blunt damage.

Hand requirement: One handed
Unique traits: Flails always deal their blunt force trauma even when missing.
This is a faction-unique weapon, it requires specialist training from the Kingdom of Numeria to gain proficiency.

Base Stats:

Hit chance: Dexterity + Proficiency
Damage: 1d4
Penetration: 2 x Dexterity (Capped by 2 x Veterancy)
Blunt Force Trauma: Dexterity (Min 1)
Total Range: 1m
Optimal Range: 1m
Melee Defence: 0
Weight: 2kg
Cost: 610g

Faction Unique Weapon: You can only craft this weapon's parts if you have proficiency in it or if taught by the relevant faction.

Moveset:

Values are organised as such: (Hit chance | Damage | Penetration | BFT | Range)
Values below serve as add-ons to the weapon's base stats, apart from range.
"S" or "D" refers to your strength or dexterity modifier.
"S|D" means to use either strength or dexterity modifier, whichever is higher.

  • "S|D" is always used as an enclosed term, meaning terms like "S|D+1" refers to "(S|D)+1" and not "S|(D+1)".
    +0 means no bonuses or maluses. =x means the value is set to x.
    "|" is used to denote "or", "/" is used to denote divide.

Tier 1:

Swing

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • This is your default opportunity attack.

Charge!

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | finishes at 1m)

  • Regular action + movement speed.
  • See charging rules for more details.

Quick Bash

(+0 | x1/2 | x1/2 | x1/2 | 1m)

  • Bonus action.

Tier 2:

Head Lash

(-D | +D | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Inflict dazed(1) on your opponent.

Unpredictable Screen

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Swing wildly at your target and move 1m whilst ignoring all opportunity attacks.
  • The target must still be within your optimal range at the end of the movement.

Tier 3:

Chain Striking

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 0m)

  • Passive bonus, only applies to this weapon.
  • Consecutive attacks on the same target gain +1 hit chance.
  • Hitting or missing does not matter.
  • Maximum bonus capped by your dexterity.
  • Bonus is lost on attacking another enemy, or spending 2 turns not attacking.

Wind...

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Combo starter, swing your flail in a circular manner, preparing to ...Release.
  • Combo is broken if you are moved/disabled. Moving of your own volition also counts.

...Release

(+D | x2 | x2 | x2 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Combo finisher, after Wind..., release a devastating hit, inflicting dazed(3).
  • Expert level DC.
