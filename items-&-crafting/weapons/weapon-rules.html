Weapon Rules

There are three weapon types, depending on how many hands they take to wield. With some exceptions, each weapon you wield can only attack once per turn, regardless of any actions you have remaining. (e.g. If you use a weapon's bonus action move, you cannot use any of its regular action moves and vice versa.)

One-handed

These weapons can be wielded alone or with an off-hand weapon.

Two-handed

Two-handed weapons require both hands to wielded and cannot be used alongside another weapon.

Off-handed

Off-handed weapons can be used to dual wield without penalty. They are always one-handed.

Dual Wielding

Dual wielding allows you to use both regular and bonus action for attacking in the same turn, but comes with some restrictions.

Dual wielding can only be done without penalty with a one-handed weapon and a off-handed weapon. Using two off-handed weapons also has no penalty.

Dual wielding with two one-handed weapons will remove your stat bonuses from the stats of both weapons. The exception is if you have the dual wielder trait.

When dual wielding, only the defences from one of the weapons is applied.

You may choose which weapon you want to make opportunity attacks with. You still only have 1 opportunity attack a turn.

Proficiency

Without proficiency, you gain no bonuses from your stats for the weapon.
For shields, lose -2 to the bonus melee and ranged defence (minimum of 0).
The tier 1 moveset of all weapons are always available, regardless of proficiency.

There are 3 tiers of proficiency. Higher tiers can be unlocked when levelling up. You gain your weapon proficiency tier in hit chance. The following stats are not cumulative.

  • Tier 1: Allows you to use the weapon with your stat bonuses. Gives+1 hit chance.
  • Tier 2: Intermediate, provides additional utility moves. Gives +2 hit chance.
  • Tier 3: Advanced, provides unique and combo moves. Gives +3 hit chance. Roll minor complications instead of major ones on natural 1s.
    Unarmed proficiency works differently.
  • Tier 1: Gives +1 hit chance and +1 to grappling checks and DCs.
  • Tier 2: Gives +2 hit chance and +2 to grappling checks and DCs (stacks with previous tiers).
  • Tier 3: Gives +3 hit chance and +3 to grappling checks and DCs. Roll minor complications instead of major one on nat 1s.

Bear in mind weapon proficiencies require the previous tier. A tier 3 proficiency requires you have tier 2 proficiency, and a tier 2 proficiency requires a tier 1 proficiency.

Some weapons require special training from their specific faction to gain tier 1 proficiency. Afterwards, you gain tier 2 and 3 proficiency as normal.
The melee weapons are:

  • One-handed Flails / Two-handed Flails (Kingdom of Numeria)
  • One-handed Falces / Two-handed Falces (Wingard Collective)
  • Moonspades (Aquatine Empire)
  • Numerian Blades (Conclave of the Steel Faeries)
  • Berserker Chain (Steelscale Clan)
    The ranged weapons are:
  • Arbalest (Kingdom of Numeria)
  • Hand Crossbows (Wingard Collective)
  • Repeating Crossbow (Aquatine Empire)
  • Muskets / Pistols (Conclave of the Steel Faeries)
  • Amber Lances (Steelscale Clan)

Ammunition

Ranged weapons require ammunition to use.
Thrown weapons don't require separate ammunition, the weapon itself is the ammunition.

By default, you can recover up to 50% of spent ammunition if you spend time scouring the battlefield after.

  • Firearm Shot cannot be recovered.
  • Certain types of ammunition, such as gunpowder-type ammunitions, also cannot be recovered.
  • Thrown weapons can be 100% recovered.

Equipping and Switching Weapons

You begin combat with your weapon loadout set. To switch/equip weapons during combat, you must use the manage items action. For thrown weapons like Javelins, throwing them will automatically "reload" another one into your hand if available in your equipment load.
