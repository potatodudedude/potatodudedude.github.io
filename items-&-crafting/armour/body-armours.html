Body Armours

This list contains common armour types available for sale.

See Armour Rules for more information on how armour is worn and fitted.

How to read stats

  • Some armour pieces have different variants made of different materials, denoted by "typeX/typeY/typeZ". The stats are also split X/Y/Z, with each stat corresponding to the variant in the same position.
  • Armour: How much armour the piece offers.
  • Adjustment Score: How difficult the armour piece is to fit to someone. See adjustment score rules for more information.
  • Repair/Adjust Material: What specific material is used to repair or adjust armour. See the armour smithing page for more information on repairing/adjusting armour.
  • Armour Customisation Tree: Shows the customisations made in accordance with the Armour Smithing system. Following this tree should allow you to replicate all the armour's stats using the system.
  • Some armours are marked as "XX-Forged". This means the armour requires special conditions to forge/refit.
    • Pixie-Metal-Forged: Needs a pixie with metal mastery or pixie smithing tools.
    • Pixie-Counter-Forged: Needs a pixie with counter mastery or pixie smithing tools.
  • Weight, Cost: Self-explanatory.
