Armour Smithing

General Overview and Rules

How to smith armour

  1. Choose which type of armour piece you want to smith below.
  2. Go down the list of customisations for that piece. You must choose 1 and only 1 option for each customisation.
  3. Add the modifiers from the customisations to the base stats.
  4. Spend resources to smith the armour during rests.

Requirements

To do anything listed below, you require the smithing(armour) skill, alongside a set of smithing tools. Some customisations may also require a pixie with certain masteries alongside the smithing tools and skill, or pixie smithing tools alongside the skill.

Customisation stat interactions

  • A piece's armour value cannot fall below 0.
  • A piece's adjustment score cannot fall below 0.
  • Percentage modifiers are additive. (i.e. two +50% modifiers stack to be +100%)
  • Weight cannot be reduced below 1kg.
  • Round up all calculations to the next whole number, except weight, which is rounded up to the next 0.25kg interval.

Armour cost calculations

Each armour piece's final cost is derived as such:
(Total Cost of Material) x (Time-units taken x 0.20) x (Customisation Modifier)

Smelting/Scrapping

You can smelt down or scrap an armour piece to get back 50% of the materials used to make them. The time-unit cost and skill requirement to scrap an armour part is equal to the tier of the material the armour is made of. (e.g. A piece made of tier 1 materials needs skill 1 in armour smithing and 1 time-unit to scrap.)

Adjusting Armour

Armour can be readjusted to fit another person to avoid incurring fitting penalties. You will need the relevant materials and time units equal to the armour's adjustment score.

If not otherwise stated, the requirements is as such:

  • 1 Adjustment Score: no skill needed, no material needed, no smithing kit needed.
  • 2 Adjustment Score: Level 1 skill + 2 material units.
  • 3 Adjustment Score: Level 2 skill + 3 material units.
  • 4+ Adjustment Score: Level 3 skill + 4 material units.

Repairing Armour

Repairing a damaged piece of armour takes half the resources and time it took to make it. The same skill and tool requirements apply.

Skill Bonuses

For each level of armour smithing above 3, the time-unit cost to make a piece of armour is reduced by 1. This time-cost reduction is not used in calculating the price of an armour piece.

Layer 1:

Armour Piece - Padding:

Base Stats:

Armour: 0
Adjustment Score: 2
Weight: 2kg
Material Cost: 4 fabric units
Craft Time: 4 time-units

Customisation - Material:

  • Medium Grade Fabric (Skill 2)
    • Standard material, no effects.
  • Low Grade Fabric (Skill 1)
    • +1kg weight.
    • -1 adjustment score.
    • -50% time-unit cost.
  • High Grade Fabric (Skill 3)
    • -1kg weight.
    • +1 adjustment score.
    • +100% time-unit cost.

Customisation - Weave:

  • Standard (Skill 1)
    • Standard weave, no effects.
  • Silent Weave (Skill 2)
    • +2 to stealth.
    • +1 adjustment score.
    • +50% time-unit cost.
  • Harness Weave (Skill 3)
    • +2kg to equipment load thresholds.
    • +2 adjustment score.
    • +50% time-unit cost.
  • Steel-Linen Weave (Skill 3)
    • Makes the piece pixie-metal-forged, needs a pixie with metal mastery or pixie smithing tools.
    • +1 armour.
    • +1 adjustment score.
    • +50% time-unit cost.
