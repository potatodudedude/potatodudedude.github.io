Ammunition Crafting

This page details crafting system for ammunitions, including Bolts, Arrows, Firearm Shot, Stones and some Throwables.

Ammunition

  • Each craftable here has many customisations available, you must pick 1 and only 1 option per customisation.
  • Next to some choices is a bracket with the prerequisites needed to craft that customisation.
  • For stat modifiers like damage, penetration etc. These act on whatever weapon uses the ammunition.
  • For stat modifiers like craft-time, material cost, weight etc. These act on the base stats of the crafting recipe.

Arrows/Bolts

Arrow/Bolts Cost Calculations

Each bundle of ammunition's final cost is derived as such:
(Total Cost of Materials) x (Time-units taken x 0.1)

Recipe Base Stats

Material Cost: 1 metal unit, 1 wood unit, 1 leather unit
Craft-Time: 4 time-units
Craft Amount: 5
Weight: 5x0.1kg

Customisation - Arrowhead Material:

This customisation determines what the metal unit used is.

  • Steel (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • Standard material, no effects.
  • Iron (Weapon-Smithing 1, Smithing Tools)
    • -1 penetration. (Double for slow-firing weapons)
    • -1 damage. (Double for slow-firing weapons)
    • -25% craft time.
  • Pixie-Forged Steel (Weapon-Smithing 3, Smithing Tools)
    • +1 damage. (Double for slow-firing weapons)
    • +25% craft time.
  • Nebulan Starshard (Weapon-Smithing 3, Smithing Tools)
    • +2 penetration. (+3 for slow-firing weapons)
    • +25% craft time.
  • No Arrowhead
    • Does not need metal unit.
    • -50% damage.
    • -25% craft time.
  • Padded Arrowhead
    • Does not need metal unit.
    • -75% damage.
    • -25% craft time.

Customisation - Arrowhead Shape:

  • Standard (Weapon-Smithing 1, Smithing Tools)
    • Standard shape, no effects.
  • Broad (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • +2 damage. (+3 for slow-firing weapons)
    • -3 penetration. (-4 for slow-firing weapons)
    • +25% craft time.
  • Piercing (Weapon-Smithing 3, Smithing Tools)
    • +3 penetration. (+4 for slow-firing weapons)
    • -2 damage. (-3 for slow-firing weapons)
    • +25% craft time.
  • Serrated (Weapon-Smithing 3, Smithing Tools)
    • Inflict bleeding(2) on hit. (Bleeding(3) for slow-firing weapons)
    • -1 penetration. (Double for slow-firing weapons)
    • +25% craft time.

Customisation - Shaft Material:

This customisation determines what the wood unit used is.

  • Medium Grade Wood (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • Standard, no effects.
  • Scrap Wood (Weapon-Smithing 1)
    • -25% craft time.
    • This ammunition is always destroyed on use and cannot be recovered.
  • High Grade Wood (Weapon-Smithing 3, Smithing Tools)
    • +25% craft time.
    • -50% weight.

Customisation - Shaft Construction:

  • Standard (Weapon-Smithing 1)
    • Standard, no effects.
  • Hollow (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • -1 damage. (Double for slow-firing weapons)
    • +2m optimal range.
    • +25% craft time.
  • Whistling (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • The arrow produces a whistling sound.
      • No direct combat effect but could have impact in roleplay depending on how you use it.
    • +25% craft time.

Customisation - Fletching Material:

This customisation determines what the leather unit used is.

  • Medium Grade Leather (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • Standard, no effects.
  • Low Grade Leather (Weapon-Smithing 1)
    • -1 hit chance.
    • -25% craft time.
  • High Grade Leather (Weapon-Smithing 3, Smithing Tools)
    • +1 hit chance.
    • +25% craft time.

Customisation - Fletching Shape:

  • Standard (Weapon-Smithing 1)
    • Standard shape, no effects.
  • Helical (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • +1 hit chance.
    • -1m optimal range.
  • Low Profile (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • -1 hit chance.
    • +1m optimal range.

Customisation - Add-Ons:

  • Nothing
    • No add ons, no effects.
  • Gunpowder Payload
    • Arrow/Bolt cannot be recovered.
    • Requires a gunpowder unit. The gunpowder's explosion will act as an additional attack on the tile where the projectile lands.
      • The explosion's potency is halved or reduced by 1, whichever is the bigger penalty.
      • On a miss, roll a d8 to see which adjacent tile the explosion is centred on.
    • +25% craft time.
  • Rocket Booster
    • Arrow/Bolt cannot be recovered.
    • Requires a gunpowder unit.
      • +1d4 blunt force trauma per level of potency.
        • +1d4 damage instead if using lesser gunpowder.
      • -1 hit chance per level of potency.
      • +1m optimal range per level of potency.
      • The gunpowder's own damage/blunt force trauma stats and status effects are not inflicted.
    • +25% craft time.

Stones

Stones Cost Calculations

Each bundle of ammunition's final cost is derived as such:
(Total Cost of Materials) x (Time-units taken x 0.2)

Recipe Base Stats

Material Cost: 1 metal unit
Craft-Time: 1 time-units
Craft Amount: 5
Weight: 5x0.1kg

Customisation - Material:

This customisation determines what the metal unit used is.

  • Stones
    • Does not require metal units.
    • The monetary value of this ammunition is set to 0.
    • Standard material, no effect.
  • Iron (Weapon-Smithing 1, Smithing Tools)
    • +1m optimal range.
    • +200% craft time.
  • Steel (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • +1m optimal range.
    • +1 penetration. (Double for slow-firing weapons)
    • +300% craft time.
  • Pixie-Forged Steel (Weapon-Smithing 3, Smithing Tools)
    • +2m optimal range.
    • +1 penetration. (Double for slow-firing weapons)
    • +400% craft time.

Customisation - Shape:

  • Standard
    • Standard shape, no effect.
  • Almond (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • +1 hit chance.
    • +200% craft time.

Customisation - Filler:

  • Nothing
    • Standard filler, no effect.
  • Whistling (Weapon-Smithing 2, Smithing Tools)
    • The stone produces a whistling sound.
      • No direct combat effect but could have impact in roleplay depending on how you use it.
    • +100% craft time.
  • Gunpowder Filler
    • Cannot be used with stone material.
    • Requires a gunpowder unit. The gunpowder's explosion will act as an additional attack on the tile where the projectile lands.
      • The explosion's potency is halved or reduced by 1, whichever is the bigger penalty.
      • On a miss, roll a d8 to see which adjacent tile the explosion is centred on.
    • +100% craft time.
