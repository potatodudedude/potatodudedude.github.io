Alchemy

General Overview and Rules

Alchemy is used to make potions/salves that cover a wide range of effects: from buffs and poisons to healing. You need an alchemy set to brew potions.

Requirements

All alchemy requires an alchemy set and alchemy skill. Some will also require a pixie with certain masteries alongside an alchemy set and skills, or a pixie alchemy set alongside the skills.

Base Ingredient and Essence

These are the main ingredients used in alchemy, and they will be abbreviated as such:

  • Albino Beetle: Essence A, or just "A"
  • Blackriver Berry: Essence B, or just "B"
  • Celusian Flower: Essence C, or just "C"
  • Medical Herbs: Essence M, or just "M"
  • Faery Reagents: Essence F, or just "F"
  • Poisonous Stuff: Essence P, or just "P"
  • Sulfur: Essence S, or just "S"
    Potions/Salves are made out of a combination of these essences.
  • Each 1 of the ingredients listed above provides 1 essence.
  • You can also use essence from their derivatives in the Medicine, Coatings and Throwables pages.

Essence Capacity

Essence capacity represents how much essence you can stuff into a potion before it becomes chemically unstable. This is primarily determined by the quality of the bottle.

Reactions

A reaction is an effect/multiple effects a potion can have. Each reaction needs a certain number and type of essence to perform. There's only a limited amount of reactions that you can put in a potion, depending on the bottle. A potion cannot have duplicate reactions.

How to make a potion

Potion making follows these steps:

  1. Prepare what bottle and essences you have available.
  2. Select the type and number of reactions you want in your potion that fit your bottle and essence restrictions.
  3. Add up the time-unit and essence cost of your reactions and spend the resources to make the potion.
    • You can use other potions, medicine, coatings or throwables as ingredients, but with some limitations:
      • You must use all of the item, you cannot separate out specific essences to use.
      • If the item contains essence that isn't included in the recipe or has essence that exceeds the amount in the recipe, it cannot be used.
  4. Calculate the monetary cost of the potion if you're planning on selling it.

Potion Stats

These are the default stats potions use, unless otherwise stated.

  • Weight: 1kg
  • Bonus action to drink.
  • Bonus action to apply as coating.
    • By default, coating a weapon with a potion will not allow you to apply the effects on hit. You need specific reactions for that.
  • Regular action to throw, DC12 dexterity, 6m range.
    • Throwing a potion will destroy the bottle.
    • By default, throwing a potion will not allow you to apply the effects on hit. You need specific reactions for that.
    • Failing the throw will result in the potion landing in one of the adjacent tiles, determined by a d8 roll.
  • The safe daily amount of a potion is determined by the lowest value of all the reactions. Taking more than the safe daily amount needs an increasingly difficult addiction check.
  • Each potion can only be used once.
  • Unless destroyed, potion bottles can be reused once the potion is expended.
  • Monetary value of a potion is decided as such:
    • Sum of all reaction costs + bottle cost.
    • A reaction's cost is determined as such: (Total Cost of Essences) x (Time-units taken).
    • Total cost of essences is based off the essence's base ingredient costs.
    • If your time-units taken is 0, change it to 1 for the value calculations.

Healing Reactions

Reaction - Health Recovery:

Salve

This reaction has the unique option to be turned into a salve instead of a potion.
Choosing to make this reaction a salve will have the following effects:

  • Change bonus "drinking" action to a regular "apply" action.
  • Remove ability to coat weapons.
  • Remove ability to throw potion.
  • Increase time-unit cost by 1.
  • Gain bonus effects dependent on the reaction tier.

Tier 1

Requirements: Alchemy 1
Effects:

  • +1d4 health.
    Salve Bonus Effects:
  • Normal healing effect becomes +1d6 health instead.
  • Removes bleeding(2).
    Essence Cost: 1H
    Safe Daily Amount: No limit.
    Time-Unit Cost: 0

Tier 2

Requirements: Alchemy 2
Effects:

  • +1d6 health.
    Salve Bonus Effects:
  • Normal healing effect becomes +1d8 health instead.
  • Removes bleeding(3).
    Essence Cost: 2H
    Safe Daily Amount: No limit.
    Time-Unit Cost: 1

Tier 3

Requirements: Alchemy 3, Pixie with Counter Mastery or Pixie Alchemy Set
Effects:

  • +2d6 health.
    Salve Bonus Effects:
  • Normal healing effect becomes +3d6 health instead.
  • Removes all bleeding.
    Essence Cost: 3H, 1F
    Safe Daily Amount: No limit.
    Time-Unit Cost: 2

Marander Filters

Having at least 1 in alchemy skill and an alchemy set allows you to make 1 marander filter with the following recipe:

  • 2 medical herbs + 1 medium grade fabric => 1 marander filter.
  • Takes 2 time-units to craft.
