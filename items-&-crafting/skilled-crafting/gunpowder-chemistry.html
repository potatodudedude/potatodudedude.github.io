Gunpowder Chemistry

General Overview and Rules

Gunpowder chemistry covers the making of gunpowder, and the making of consumables that use gunpowder. Gunpowder is considered contraband, so beware of using it carelessly, especially for non-faery players.

Requirements

You need an alchemy set and gunpowder chemistry skill as a bare minimum to do any gunpowder chemistry. Some chemistry will also require a pixie with certain masteries alongside an alchemy set and skills, or a pixie alchemy set alongside the skills.

Gunpowder

Customisable gunpowder can be made, with differing effects depending on the ingredient composition. Each gunpowder unit is made with these main ingredients:

  • Faery Reagents: Essence F, or just "F"
  • Saltpeter: Essence SP, or just "SP"
  • Sulfur: Essence S, or just "S"
  • High Grade Wood: Essence W, or just "W"
    Each 1 of the ingredients listed above provides 1 essence.

How to craft gunpowder

  1. Choose whether to make lesser or true gunpowder. Both share the same customisations.
  2. Go down the list of gunpowder customisations. You must choose 1 and only 1 option for each customisation.
  3. Add the modifiers from the customisations to the base stats.
  4. Spend resources to make gunpowder during rests.

Gunpowder Cost Calculations

Each gunpowder unit's final cost is derived as such:
(Total Cost of Essence) x (Time-units taken x 0.50)
Total cost of essence uses the base ingredient costs.

Gunpowder Base Stats

Gunpowder stats have something called "potency", along side "XXX per potency". Simply multiply these together to get the final stat. If potency is reduced to 0, the gunpowder does no damage/blunt force trauma, but still inflicts statuses as though it had a potency of 1.

If used by itself, gunpowder needs a regular action to activate, loses 1 potency and can only be used on the user's tile in a 1m radius.

Lesser Gunpowder:

  • Requirements: Gunpowder Chemistry 1
  • Potency: 2
  • Damage per potency: 1d6
  • Penetration per potency: 2
  • Area of Effect: 1m radius around target tile.
  • Essence cost: 2S, 2SP, 2W
  • Time-unit cost: 2
  • Weight: 1kg

True Gunpowder:

  • Requirements: Gunpowder Chemistry 3, pixie with gunpowder mastery or pixie alchemy set.
  • Potency: 2
  • Blunt Force Trauma per potency: 1d6
  • Inflicts shellshock(1) per 2 potency, DC15 + Gunpowder Chemistry Skill.
  • Area of Effect: 1m radius around target tile.
  • Essence cost: 2S, 2SP, 2W, 1F
  • Craft time: 2 time-units
  • Weight: 1kg

Gunpowder Customisations

Customisation - Combustion Rate:

  • Standard Rate (Gunpowder Chemistry 1)
    • No effects.
  • Slow Burning (Gunpowder Chemistry 2)
    • -1 potency.
    • +1SP essence cost.
    • +1m area of effect radius.
  • Fast Burning (Gunpowder Chemistry 3)
    • +1 potency.
    • +1S essence cost.
    • -1m area of effect radius.
  • Unstable Powder (Gunpowder Chemistry 3)
    • +2 potency.
    • +1SP, 1S essence cost.
    • Make a dexterity DC5 check every time this powder or an consumable made from this powder is handled. Failing this will result in the item detonating on the user's tile.
  • Ultimatum (Gunpowder Chemistry 3, pixie with gunpowder mastery or pixie alchemy set)
    • +4 potency.
    • +2m area of effect radius,
    • Shellshock effect is guaranteed.
    • +6F essence cost.
    • +4 time-unit cost.

Customisation - Extra Effects:

  • Standard (Gunpowder Chemistry 1)
    • No effects.
  • Incendiary (Gunpowder Chemistry 2)
    • Inflict burning(1) per potency, DC15 + Gunpowder Chemistry Skill.
    • +1W essence cost.
    • +1 time-unit cost.
  • Corrosive (Gunpowder Chemistry 2)
    • Inflict corroded(1) per potency, DC15 + Gunpowder Chemistry Skill.
    • +1S essence cost.
    • +1 time-unit cost.
  • Blinding (Gunpowder Chemistry 3)
    • Inflict blinded(1) per potency, DC15 + Gunpowder Chemistry Skill.
    • +1S, 1SP essence cost.
    • +2 time-unit cost.

Grenades

How to craft grenades

  1. Choose a gunpowder unit. Its stats will be the explosion stats for the grenade.
  2. Go down the list of grenade customisations. You must choose 1 and only 1 option for each customisation.
  3. Add the modifiers from the customisations to the base stats.
  4. Spend resources to make the grenade during rests.

Grenade Cost Calculations

Each grenade's final cost is derived as such:
(Total Cost of Materials) x (Time-units taken x 0.25)

Grenade Base Stats

  • Throw Range: 5m
  • Throw dexterity DC: 12
  • Damage and blast radius dependent on gunpowder used.
  • Material Cost: 4 metal units, 1 gunpowder unit.
  • Craft time: 4 time-units

Grenade Customisations

Customisation - Casing Material

This determines what the metal units used are.

  • Steel (Weapon-Smithing 2, smithing tools)
    • Standard material, no effect.
  • Iron (Weapon-Smithing 1, smithing tools)
    • -1m range.
    • -1 time-unit cost.
  • Pixie-Forged Steel (Weapon-Smithing 3, smithing tools)
    • +1m range.
    • +1 time-unit cost.
  • Bottles
    • Does not need metal units, uses 1 clay, glass or crystal bottles instead.
    • +1m range.
    • -1 potency.
    • -2 time-unit cost.

Customisation - Casing Construction

  • Standard (Weapon-Smithing 1, smithing tools)
    • Standard construction, no effect.
  • Fragmentation (Weapon-Smithing 3, smithing tools)
    • Anyone in a radius 1m more than the grenade's is hit by shrapnel.
    • Shrapnel deals 1d4 damage with 0 penetration.
    • The number of shrapnel inflicted is equal to the potency of the grenade.
    • Each individual shrapnel is counted as its own attack.
    • +2 time-unit cost.
  • Dart (Weapon-Smithing 3, smithing tools)
    • +2m range.
    • -7 throw DC.
    • +2 time-unit cost.

Customisation - Fuse Timer

  • Standard
    • Standard fuse time, grenade explodes on landing.
  • Long Fuse
    • Grenade explodes at the start or the end of the next round.
      • This must be decided before throwing and cannot be changed after.
    • +1W essence cost.
  • Unpredictable Fuse
    • Roll a d4 at the below points. The grenade explodes on a 1.
      • On initial landing.
      • At the start of every subsequent zig-zag cycle.
    • The grenade will detonate at the end of the next round, regardless of the d4 roll.

Firearm Shot

How to craft firearm shots

  1. Choose a gunpowder unit. Its stats will be stats for the firearm shot with some caveats, see below.
  2. Go down the list of firearm shot customisations. You must choose 1 and only 1 option for each customisation.
  3. Add the modifiers from the customisations to the base stats.
  4. Spend resources to make the firearm shots during rests.

Firearm Shot Cost Calculations

Each gunpowder unit's final cost is derived as such:
(Total Cost of Materials) x (Time-units taken x 0.5)

Firearm Shot Base Stats

Unlike grenades, firearm shot does not use all the stats from the gunpowder unit directly.

  • Blunt Force Trauma is determined by the weapon's stats.
    • For each level of potency above 2, gain +2 blunt force trauma.
    • For each level of potency below 2, lose -2 blunt force trauma.
    • For lesser gunpowder, convert blunt force trauma to regular damage with 4 penetration.
    • Regular damage bonuses from paths, traits etc. are applied to blunt force trauma if using true gunpowder.
  • Firearm shots do not use the area of effect stat.
  • Status effects still apply, but use the weapon's normal saving throw DC calculations.
  • Crafting amount: 3
  • Craft time: 2 time-units
  • Material Cost: 2 metal units, 1 gunpowder unit.
  • Weight: 3 x 0.1kg

Firearm Shot Customisations

Customisation - Bullet Material:

This determines what the metal units used are.

  • Steel (Weapon-Smithing 2, smithing tools)
    • Standard material, no effect.
  • Iron (Weapon-Smithing 1, smithing tools)
    • -2 blunt force trauma.
    • -1 time-unit cost.
  • Pixie-Forged Steel (Weapon-Smithing 3, smithing tools)
    • +2 blunt force trauma.
    • +1 time-unit cost.

Customisation - Bullet Shape:

  • Round (Weapon-Smithing 1, smithing tools)
    • Standard shape, no effects.
  • Needle (Weapon-Smithing 3, smithing tools)
    • Hit chance: Adv
    • Optimal Range: +5m
    • Blunt Force Trauma: x1/2
    • Maximum range is doubled.
    • +2 time-unit cost.
  • Grapeshot (Weapon-Smithing 3, smithing tools)
    • Take the maximum possible blunt force trauma the round shot customisation would do out of the weapon and divide by 3 to get the number of pellets in the grapeshot. (Rounded up)
    • For each pellet, deal 1d6 blunt force trauma.
    • -5 hit chance.
    • Maximum range is cut to a quarter its original.
    • +2 time-unit cost.

Customisation - Gunpowder Load

  • Standard
    • Standard load, no effects.
  • Under-loaded
    • -50% blunt force trauma.
    • Loading this ammunition does not need an action.
    • +2 crafted amount.
  • Over-loaded
    • +50% blunt force trauma.
    • Firing this ammunition requires a d4 roll.
      • If this roll is greater than the level of material used in the firearm's barrel part, the barrel explodes and is damaged, dealing half the blunt force damage to the user.
      • The shot is still fired in case of an overload explosion.
    • -1 crafted amount.
