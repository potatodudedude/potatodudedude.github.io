Dogs

Dogs serve as companions alongside your party, and can impose certain benefits alongside them. There are several different breeds of dogs, each with their own advantages and disadvantages. Dogs always come with the weasley miscreant trait, and are always considered under light load.

Dogs cannot carry items or perform skill checks for you.

Breeds

Guard Dogs

These dogs serve as early warning systems, informing you of impending enemies and excelling in hunting vermin. They are bred for their sensitive noses, but not their strength or power, so be wary of sending them head first into battle. Think of terriers and dachshunds in our world.

Stats

Health: 10
Armour: 0
Movement Speed: 5m
Jump Distance: 3m
Melee Defense: 14
Ranged Defense: 14
Initiative: 4

Strength: 1
Dexterity: 1
Endurance: 1

Cost: 250g
Requires 1 unit of food a day.

Bonuses

  • Grants advantage on scouting checks.

Herd Dogs

These dogs are commonly used in herding, possessing great stamina and intelligence. They are a littler stockier than guard dogs, and can hold their own better in combat. Think of breeds like the german shepard or the collie border in our world. Herd dogs require 50% less time-units to train.

Stats

Health: 12
Armour: 0
Movement Speed: 7m
Jump Distance: 3m
Melee Defence: 16
Ranged Defence: 16
Initiative: 6

Strength: 2
Dexterity: 2
Endurance: 2

Cost: 500g
Requires 1 unit of food a day.

Bonuses

  • Grants +3 on scouting checks.
    • This allows scouting skill to go above the normal cap of 3.
  • Training time-unit cost is halved.

War Dogs

These dogs are bred for war or killing predators. They are large with deadly bites and thick fur, requiring more food than other breeds. Think of breeds like the cane corso or the tibetan mastiff in our world.

Stats

Health: 15
Armour: 0
Movement Speed: 6m
Jump Distance: 3m
Melee Defense: 14
Ranged Defense: 14
Initiative: 4

Strength: 3
Dexterity: 1
Endurance: 2

Cost: 1000g
Requires 2 units of food a day.

Bonuses

  • Grants +2 on scouting checks.
    • This allows scouting skill to go above the normal cap of 3.

Moves

Base Stats:

Hit chance: +Strength
Damage: 1d4 + Strength
Penetration: 0
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Total Range: 1m
Optimal Range: 1m

Default Moves

Maul

(+0 | +0 | +0 | +0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Can be used when grappled.
  • This is your default opportunity attack.

Trained Moves

These moves require you to train the dog before it can perform them.

Hold

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Grappling move.
  • Grapples the opponent.
  • If the target is wielding a melee weapon with 1m optimal range and is not flanked, they will get an opportunity attack. This does not stop the grapple attempt if it hits.
  • Uses strength/dexterity for DC.
  • Training time cost: 4 time-units.

Free

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Performs the free action on an ally.
  • Training time cost: 4 time-units.

Fetch

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Regular action.
  • Allows the dog to pick up and carry 1 item under 5kg in weight.
  • Training time cost: 8 time-units.

Agility Drills

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Passive.
  • The dog gains +2 to its defences.
  • Training time cost: 8 time-units.

Hunting Prowess

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Passive.
  • The dog provides +3 to hunting checks.
    • This allows hunting skill to go above the normal cap of 3.
  • Training time cost: 8 time-units.

Savage Mauler

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Passive.
  • The dog's strength modifier is added twice when calculating damage.
  • Training time cost: 8 time-units.

Do a trick!

(=0 | =0 | =0 | =0 | 1m)

  • Passive.
  • The dog provides +2 to persuasion checks.
    • This allows persuasion skill to go above the normal cap of 3.
  • Training time cost: 8 time-units.
