Horse Equipment

Barding

Each horse can only wear 1 barding at a time.

Half-Bardings

  • All bardings have a half variant, which has half the weight and costs, but only provides armour when attacked from the front.
  • "From the front" refers to attacks from enemies in the horse's view cone, or non-flanking attacks.

Utility

Cart

  • Multiplies horses' carry capacity by 2.
  • Allows 2 people to ride in the cart.
  • Removes ability to ride horse in combat.
  • Repair/Adjust Material: Medium Grade Wood
  • Crafting Skill: Armour Smithing 1
  • Material Cost: 8
  • Craft Time: 8
  • Weight: 50kg
    • The cart's weight does not count towards the horse's carrying capacity.
  • Cost: 320g

Wagon

  • Multiplies horses' carry capacity by 4.
  • Allows 5 people to ride in the cart.
  • Removes ability to ride horse in combat.
  • Repair/Adjust Material: Medium Grade Wood
  • Crafting Skill: Armour Smithing 2
  • Material Cost: 16
  • Craft Time: 8
  • Weight: 100kg
    • The cart's weight does not count towards the horse's carrying capacity.
  • Cost: 640g

Cargo Wagon

  • Multiplies horses' carry capacity by 8.
  • Allows 10 people to ride in the cart.
  • Removes ability to ride horse in combat.
  • Requires at least 2 horses to pull.
  • Repair/Adjust Material: Medium Grade Wood
  • Crafting Skill: Armour Smithing 3
  • Material Cost: 16
  • Craft Time: 12
  • Weight: 300kg
    • The cart's weight does not count towards the horse's carrying capacity.
  • Cost: 960g
