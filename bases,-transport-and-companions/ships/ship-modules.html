Weapons (Large)

Ballista

Health: 30
Armour: 5
Repair requirements: Timber & Carpentry
Module Size: Large
Hit chance: +Int
Damage: 3d6 + 6
Penetration: 10
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Optimal Range: 50m
Range Penalty:
Hit chance: -1
Damage: -1
Penetration: -1
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Maximum Penalty Count: 4
Total Range: 300m
Cost: 2000g

  • Disadvantage to hit against humanoid or smaller targets.
  • Pierces up to 3m behind the target. Targets taking this collateral damage take half damage.
  • On a miss, the impact hits a tile 1m away, determined by a d8 roll.
  • Reloading is not free and takes a turn.
  • Uses Javelins/Spears as ammunition.

Hwacha

Health: 20
Armour: 5
Repair requirements: Timber & Carpentry
Module Size: Large
Hit chance: -5
Damage: (1d6) * 1d6
Penetration: 5
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Optimal Range: 25m
Range Penalty:
Hit chance: -1
Damage: -1
Penetration: -1
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Maximum Penalty Count: 4
Total Range: 150m
Cost: 2000g

  • Similar to how Throwables work, the user makes an DC15 int check. Failing will result in the targeting being up to 2m off, determined by a d8 roll and a coin flip.
  • The hwacha covers an area with a radius of 3m with a hail of arrows. Each person in the area is then has to defend against 1d6 arrows and is suppressed(1, DC15).
  • Reloading is not free and takes a turn.
  • Uses Bolts/Arrows. Special ammunition gain the advantage of their effects, but only if the entire salvo is comprised of the special ammunition.
  • There are 3 types of salvos that can be fired:
    • Full load: Requires 30 bolts/arrows.
    • 2/3 load: Requires 20 bolts/arrows. -1m to effect radius, -1 to number of projectiles per tile.
    • 1/3 load: Requires 10 bolts/arrows -2m to effect radius, -2 to number of projectiles per tile.

Catapult

Health: 20
Armour: 0
Repair requirements: Timber & Carpentry
Module Size: Large
Hit chance: -
Damage: 0
Penetration: 0
Blunt Force Trauma: 3d6
Minimum Range: 50m
Total Range: 150m
Cost: 2000g

  • Does not require line of sight or suffer from distance penalties, but has a minimum firing distance.
  • Hit location is determined by the player physically throwing a d20 onto the map. Distance determined by DM. Players can make a DC15 Int check to throw with advantage.
  • Reloading is not free and takes a turn.
  • Can launch heavy stones, weighing 10kg each. These deal damage in a 1m radius. These are free, but must be loaded on your ship.
  • Can launch up to 3 Throwables. These throwables will have their splash radius extended by 1m. The user will need to throw the same number of d20s onto the map at the same time to determine hit location.

Net Launcher

Health: 20
Armour: 0
Repair requirements: Cloth/Linen/Wool & Tailoring
Module Size: Large
Damage: 0
Penetration: 0
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Total Range: 50m
Cost: 200g

  • Similar to how Throwables work, the user makes an DC15 int check. Failing will result in the targeting being up to 2m off, determined by a d8 roll and a coin flip.
  • The launcher covers an area with a radius of 2m with a net. People inside are tangled.
  • When shot at a ship, removes the bonuses from up to 3 of their sails until they are each released using the free action.
  • Reloading is not free and takes a turn.
  • Ammunition is free and has no weight.

Mortar

Health: 30
Armour: 15
Repair requirements: Metal Bars & Smithing
Module Size: Large
Hit chance: -
Damage: 0
Penetration: 0
Blunt Force Trauma: 3d6 + 12
Minimum Range: 50m
Total Range: 300m
Cost: 20000g

  • Does not require line of sight or suffer from distance penalties, but has a minimum firing distance.
  • Hit location is determined by the player physically throwing a d20 onto the map. Distance determined by DM. Players have 2 chances to throw and can make a DC12 Int check for a third chance.
  • Reloading is not free and takes a turn.
  • Uses Cannon Shot, but cannot use chain shot or grapeshot.
  • Solid shot deals it's damage in a 1m radius AOE.
  • Explosive shots have their damage radius expanded to 2m/4m.

Wingardian Shredder

Health: 15
Armour: 2
Repair requirements: Timber & Carpentry
Module Size: Small
Hit chance: +Dex
Damage: 1d6
Penetration: 2
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Optimal Range: 10m
Range Penalty:
Hit chance: -1
Damage: -1
Penetration: -1
Blunt Force Trauma: 0
Maximum Penalty Count: 4
Total Range: 150m
Cost: 1000g

  • Fires in salvos of 6. Can be aimed at different targets.

Health: 30
Armour: 5
Repair requirements: Timber & Carpentry
Module Size: Large
Cost: 200g

  • Allows the ship advantage when manoeuvring to board.
  • Once dropped, the ramp prevents either ship from leaving until it is released or destroyed.
  • Requires 2 skill checks to dislodge a boarding ramp. (None if the ramp belongs to you.)

Health: 20
Armour: 0
Repair requirements: Timber & Carpentry
Module Size: Large
Cost: 200g

  • Allows you to gain advantage on scouting when stood inside.
  • You will take 2d6 fall damage if the crow's nest is destroyed with you inside.
